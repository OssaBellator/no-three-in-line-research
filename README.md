# Alternating-Core Proof Chain

**Branch:** `research/alternating-core-chain`

This is the principal dependent proof-chain branch. It develops paid structural re-extraction, finite-role concentration, alternating two-layer transitions, reverse-scale compatibility, and the prime-minus-one endpoint.

> **Status:** Every executable failed bank has an exact created-cell-rank split into ranks one, two and three. RI fixed, moving and crossed-blocker outputs carry explicit pre-transition current/new words, while dense conflicts have a finite local-role dictionary. AC3fs--AC3fv now give realized rank-one collateral a private pivot bucket and a union-safe rectangle decoder. The union audit corrected an earlier shortcut: a full two-colour phase flip preserves the rectangle union and cannot pay for a union triple. BDA5ai--BDA5ak, AC3fw--AC3fy and AC3fz--AC3gb replace it with bounded auxiliary blocker repairs. Clean and missing-support created-role alphabets are therefore `12` and `36`, with safe complete-envelope bounds `21` and `53`. The remaining work is rank-two/rank-three termination, propagation of the union-safe menu through older product ledgers, affine chains, same-role overload recursion and global no-recycling. The branch still lacks a total AC4 transition oracle.
