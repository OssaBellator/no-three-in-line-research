# Orbit-Phase Expansion Research Track

**Branch:** `research/orbit-phase-expansion`

This branch develops the alternative phase-code decoder: orbit phases become finite-alphabet variables, real triples become canonical rank-at-most-three forbidden assignments, and cyclic residual cores are routed through topology, implication, rational-quotient, carry, and protected-correction structure. Canonical proof notes are under `docs/`.

> **Status:** Forests, pseudoforests, cacti, bounded feedback distance, and bounded incidence treewidth are exactly completable. The decoder also has exact phase drift, paid-witness localization, rank-two implication routing, rank-three carry routing, state-qualified recurrence ledgers, current-syndrome payment, and one-centre descent for every nonempty current structured output. OP4p--OP4x reconstruct a paid fixed quotient edge as a common closed physical I6 bank with exact active and blocker terms. OP4y--OP4ah classify every active term by one of nine source-coset path/cycle types and compress all shifts to endpoint displacements or cycle holonomy. OP4ai--OP4al now give a unique one-edge gauge normal form: zero-holonomy cycles are completely unshifted at the quotient level, while nonzero holonomy retains its exact finite order. The remaining problem is physical payment of endpoint displacements and unshifted cycles, non-gauge-invariant owner/legality/context fields, incomplete fibres, scale outputs, bounded recurrent blocker fibres, and action-literal kernels or wide action CSPs.

## Highest-value frontier

1. Pay or absorb one endpoint-displacement path or one completely unshifted zero-holonomy physical cycle; return the least non-gauge-invariant field otherwise.
2. Classify incomplete-fibre, one-root scale-imbalance and scale-dispersion outputs from OP4s.
3. Classify bounded recurrent rank-three blocker fibres by a simultaneous absorber or completion theorem.
4. Certify or reroute repeated action-literal kernels and wide action CSPs without losing current payment.
5. Integrate those arithmetic outputs into a total decoder oracle rather than only a one-centre descent fallback.

The scripts in `scripts/` exhaust finite abstractions and small arithmetic instances; they are not substitutes for the general proofs.
