# Orbit-Phase Expansion Research Track

**Branch:** `research/orbit-phase-expansion`

This branch develops the alternative phase-code decoder: orbit phases become finite-alphabet variables, real triples become canonical rank-at-most-three forbidden assignments, and cyclic residual cores are routed through topology, implication, rational-quotient, carry, and protected-correction structure. Canonical proof notes are under `docs/`.

> **Status:** Forests, pseudoforests, cacti, bounded feedback distance, and bounded incidence treewidth are exactly completable. The decoder also has exact phase drift, paid-witness localization, rank-two implication routing, rank-three carry routing, state-qualified recurrence ledgers, current-syndrome payment, and one-centre descent for every nonempty current structured output. OP4p--OP4s reconstruct every real two-channel factor as a physical RI record and route a paid fixed quotient edge to explicit incomplete companions, one-root scale imbalance, scale dispersion, or one coherent physical fixed-edge class. OP4t--OP4x now prove that the paid roots are completion-fixed, install every I6 state through one common physical closure, repair every blocker occupancy, and return exact source-coset-rank or blocker terms when the bank fails. The remaining problem is arithmetic classification or absorption of those named terms, bounded recurrent blocker fibres, and action-literal kernels or wide action CSPs.

## Highest-value frontier

1. Classify or absorb the exact `F,C_1,C_2,C_3,B` outputs of the closed OP4w bank.
2. Classify incomplete-fibre, one-root scale-imbalance and scale-dispersion outputs from OP4s.
3. Classify bounded recurrent rank-three blocker fibres by a simultaneous absorber or completion theorem.
4. Certify or reroute repeated action-literal kernels and wide action CSPs without losing current payment.
5. Integrate those arithmetic outputs into a total decoder oracle rather than only a one-centre descent fallback.

The scripts in `scripts/` exhaust finite abstractions and small arithmetic instances; they are not substitutes for the general proofs.
