# Geometric Cleaning Research Track

**Branch:** `research/geometric-cleaning`

This branch develops the scale-sensitive cleaning route from local candidate banks to a clean-host endpoint. It is an independent research branch: branch-specific proofs live in `docs/`, executable finite checks live in `scripts/`, and `proofs/theorem-index.md` records exactly what is proved here.

> **Status:** The global no-three-in-line conjecture is not proved on this branch. The completed chain includes exact partner and pair-shadow budgets, high-load peeling, Hall-deficiency localization, destruction-faithful source transfer, created-collateral lineage chargeback, compatible current-star product neutralization, donor-reservoir routing, finite feedback dictionaries, bounded-reservoir matching/tickets, and two exact all-`n` completion endpoints. GC4m--GC4t give union-safe same-token payment or an exact Hall deficiency. GC4u--GC4y separate clean-height-safe operations from exact protected-event causes, and GC4z--GC4ac now show that occurrence-faithful protected capacities retain safe weight `W-U`; relative capacity yields explicit clean-height payment or Hall deficiency, while failure is a certified address-capacity overload. The remaining work is proving and paying actual protected-event capacities, target-common/global blocker removal, replenishable donors, recurrent untagged feedback, context causes, pool depletion, and the local superregular endpoint.
