# Geometric obstruction for aligned threshold layers

`docs/569` aligned one conservative threshold matrix, its permutation
layers, and two transient observables.  Algebraic alignment does not guarantee
that an individual permutation layer is a legal no-three-in-line board.  This
chapter performs that missing geometric audit.

The source matrix is

```text
M=((2,1,1,0),
   (0,2,1,1),
   (1,0,2,1),
   (1,1,0,2)).
```

A permutation is viewed as four cells `(i,p(i))` in a four-by-four board.

## 1. Legal permutation census

### Theorem PP3crn -- PROVED / FOUR-BY-FOUR LAYER LEGALITY

Exactly eighteen of the twenty-four permutations of four columns contain no
collinear triple.  The remaining six are geometrically illegal layers.

#### Proof

Every permutation is enumerated and all four of its three-cell subsets are
checked by the determinant test. ∎

## 2. No all-legal decomposition

### Theorem PP3cro -- PROVED / CONSERVATIVE MATRIX GEOMETRIC OBSTRUCTION

The matrix `M` has eighty-four ordered decompositions into four permutation
layers, but none uses four geometrically legal layers.  More precisely, the
number of illegal layers is distributed as

```text
2 illegal layers: 48 decompositions,
3 illegal layers: 12 decompositions,
4 illegal layers: 24 decompositions.
```

Thus every four-slot realization of this benchmark has at least two illegal
slots.

#### Proof

The exact Birkhoff recursion enumerates all eighty-four decompositions.  Applying
`PP3crn` to every layer gives the displayed distribution. ∎

## 3. Geometric residual normals

### Theorem PP3crp -- PROVED / COLLINEAR-TRIPLE NORMAL CENSUS

Among the twelve positive cells of `M`, there are fifteen collinear triples.
Their occupancy vectors across the selected aligned decomposition are genuine
geometric residual normals for the inequalities

```text
sum_(cell in triple) occupancy(cell) <= 2.
```

Eleven of these normals lie in the row space generated by the constant,
fixed-point, and cyclic-forward observables.  Four are hidden.  One hidden
witness is the triple

```text
(0,0),(1,1),(3,3)
```

with layer normal `(1,3,0,2)`.

#### Proof

The checker enumerates all collinear triples of positive cells, constructs their
four-layer occupancy vectors, and performs exact rational rank tests against the
aligned quotient. ∎


The current aligned matrix is not merely missing a normal list: it cannot be
realized by four legal permutation layers at all.  Threshold progress now
