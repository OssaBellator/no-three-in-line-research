# Exact first-host integer singleton signature atlas

**Branch:** `research/exact-recurrent-lyapunov-audit`

**Status:** full integer-lattice singleton classification proved; physical coverage and multi-point backgrounds remain open.

## Scope

The first residual host has response menu

```text
original residual responses: 3012, 3210
local reopening responses:   2031, 2310, 3201
```

For one integer background point disjoint from the union of these response points, the pair-through-response-point part of the 31-coordinate signature vanishes. Its complete score increment is therefore determined only by which of the 20 response-secant lines contain the point.

## Completeness argument

Every integer point is exactly one of:

1. off all 20 secants;
2. on exactly one secant;
3. at an intersection of at least two secants.

Every secant contains integer points because it is generated by two integer response points. A generic integer witness, avoiding all other secants and all response points, is recorded for each line.

The 20 lines have 103 distinct rational intersections. Of these, 27 are integral; 11 are response-union points and are excluded, leaving 16 admissible integer intersections. Hence the compiler exhausts every possible integer singleton incidence pattern.

## Exact census

After combining equal score-increment vectors, there are exactly

```text
15 integer singleton score signatures
14 signatures with minimum complete score 0
 1 signature with minimum complete score 1
 0 signatures with 3012 or 3210 in the minimizer face
```

The number of zero-score reopening responses per signature is

```text
three zero reopenings  3 signatures
two zero reopenings    6 signatures
one zero reopening     5 signatures
no zero reopening      1 signature
```

Thus an arbitrary integer singleton background never makes an original residual response optimal. Fourteen of the fifteen signatures retain at least one completely triple-free reopening response.

## Unique exceptional signature

The sole signature with no zero-score reopening is

```text
3012 = 2
3210 = 10
2031 = 1
2310 = 1
3201 = 1
```

It is realized by exactly two integer secant-intersection points:

```text
(-1,4), (4,-1).
```

Even in this exceptional class, all three reopening responses remain the minimizer face. What fails is zero-response dispatch, not selector dominance over the original residual responses.

## Boundary

This theorem classifies every possible one-point integer background, but it does not prove that a physical occurrence has background size one. With two or more background points, pair-through-response-point coordinates can be nonzero and singleton score increments need not simply add. The next exact target is therefore the two-point background layer or a construction theorem restricting physical backgrounds to the safe singleton/forbidden classes.

No legal-operation, recurrent-row, Lyapunov, termination, or all-`n` conclusion is claimed.
