# Complete support gives zero-cost punctured-star termination

PP3ake--PP3akj free a captive source-star centre by puncturing one controller
value.  PP3apm--PP3apr show that every strict paid puncture stage decreases a
nested chronological potential.  The remaining internal concern was insertion
cost at the star-credit scale.

The complete marked-support theorem removes that concern.  After the centre is
punctured, form the hypergraph of every positive canonical source-invalid or
insertion signature on the ordinary helper reservoir.  A sufficiently large
independent helper block gives a marked single-cycle trade with zero source
violations and zero insertion cost.  If the independent block is too small, the
maximal-independent-set theorem yields a near-linear terminal pencil, and
PP3anu--PP3aoc together with PP3aqo--PP3aqt already convert every such source or
insertion class.

Thus every internally supported captive-star puncture is a strict paid stage with
its full star credit.  Repeated punctures terminate by nested-potential
monotonicity.  Neither final-universe insertion cost nor future-controller credit
dependencies arise on this branch.

## 1. Marked complete-support hypergraph after puncturing

Let `p` be a captive source-star centre.  Puncture `p` from its controller pool and
write

```text
V^-=
{active candidate entries not controlled by p}.
```

By PP3akg, the designated star entries remain in `V^-`; let their exact incidence
count be `C>=1`.

Choose an ambient controller-disjoint helper layer `H` containing `p` as the unique
distinguished centre.  For every positive canonical source-invalid or insertion
signature compatible with a marked single cycle through `p`, record its ordinary-
helper support.  Let

```text
K_p
```

be the resulting simple support hypergraph.

### Proposition PP3arb -- PROVED

Every nonzero positive source-invalid or insertion event compatible with the marked
single-cycle law has a nonempty edge in `K_p`.  The maximum support rank is at most
five.

#### Proof

This is the one-marked-centre case of PP3aod--PP3aoh and PP3aqi.  Every nonzero
canonical event contains at least one selected off-diagonal arc, hence at least one
ordinary helper endpoint after the distinguished centre is fixed.  The only formal
zero-helper insertion classes are a diagonal and a transposition, both absent from
a single cycle.  The canonical source ranks are at most six including the marked
centre, so ordinary-helper rank is at most five. ∎

## 2. Zero-cost marked star trade

Fix a required marked state size `q`, so `q-1` ordinary helpers are needed.

### Theorem PP3arc -- PROVED / CONDITIONAL CONTROLLER-DISJOINT HELPER INTERFACE

Exactly one of the following occurs.

1. `K_p` has an independent set of size `q-1`.  Then there is a source-valid marked
   single-cycle trade moving `p` with

   ```text
   insertion cost=0.
   ```

2. There is a fixed ordinary-helper core of size at most four with at least

   ```text
   (|H|-q) / [sum_(j=0)^4 binom(q-2,j)]
   ```

   variable extensions.

For adaptive `q=|H|^(o(1))`, the extension family has size `|H|^(1-o(1))`.

#### Proof

Apply the maximal-independent helper theorem PP3ann--PP3aoo to `K_p`.  In the
independent branch, Proposition PP3arb implies that no positive source or insertion
signature is selected.  Any directed single cycle on the marked centre and the
chosen helpers moves `p`, so the trade is source-valid and has zero insertion cost.
The second branch is the maximality conclusion with support rank five. ∎

The first branch is deterministic after the helper set is selected; no
self-recapture or foreign-cost first moment remains.

## 3. Terminal-pencil branch is already closed

### Theorem PP3ard -- PROVED / CONDITIONAL EXISTING HOST INTERFACES

A terminal pencil from alternative 2 of PP3arc has one of the following outcomes.

1. A unary, transition, or anchored source pencil yields a source star, transition
   sunflower, credited anchor bank, or endpoint bank.
2. An inserted source-triple pencil of rank four, five, or six is impossible by
   line--coordinate unique completion.
3. An insertion pencil is exactly `A_2`, `B_3`, or `B_4` and enters the established
   petal/grid/fan chain, followed by robust absorption or zero-cost bounded-marked
   cancellation.
4. A controller-pool, distinguished-endpoint, source-clean, transition, Hall,
   alternating, or matching host fails explicitly.

No recursive marked-support class remains.

#### Proof

Apply the type-preservation theorem PP3aqo and then PP3aqp--PP3aqr.  The one-marked
centre is a special case of their bounded distinguished set. ∎

## 4. Full star credit gives strict nested progress

Suppose the independent branch of PP3arc occurs and produces source `S'` from the
current source `S` in the punctured universe `V^-`.

### Theorem PP3are -- PROVED

The trade satisfies

```text
Psi_(V^-)(S')
<=
Psi_(V^-)(S)-C.
```

Hence the complete puncture-plus-trade operation decreases the chronological
nested potential by at least `C`.

#### Proof

PP3akg gives removal of all `C` designated star incidences in `V^-`.  PP3arc gives
zero insertion cost and source validity.  The exact dynamic identity therefore
gives change at most `-C` in the fixed punctured universe.  Apply PP3apn to compare
with the pre-puncture potential. ∎

This is stronger than the expected marked self-recapture bound PP3akh: all selected
self-recapture and foreign insertion events are excluded simultaneously.

## 5. Repeated captive-star punctures terminate

### Theorem PP3arf -- PROVED / CONDITIONAL EXTERNAL-HOST INTERFACE

Consider any sequence of captive-star punctures.  At every stage apply PP3arc and
PP3ard.  Then after finitely many stages one reaches at least one of:

1. a ready state and final allocation;
2. robust direct completion;
3. a free or punctured source/anchor/transition endpoint bank entering an existing
   conversion;
4. a zero-cost marked trade decreasing the nested potential by its complete star
   credit;
5. an explicit external controller-pool, distinguished-endpoint, source-clean,
   transition, Hall, alternating, matching, or controller-preservation failure.

A positive puncture reserve cannot be exhausted by internally supported stages.

#### Proof

Every independent branch is a strict nested-paid stage by PP3are.  Such stages are
finite by PP3apo.  Every terminal-pencil branch is converted or external by
PP3ard.  The direct alternatives are inherited from PP3aki and the initial robust
allocation chain. ∎

## 6. Elimination of the internal history costs

### Corollary PP3arg -- PROVED

On the complete-support captive-star branch:

```text
current-universe insertion cost=0
```

at every puncture stage.  Consequently:

1. every stage is strict paid in its post-puncture universe;
2. nested-potential monotonicity terminates the process;
3. no final-universe insertion balance is needed;
4. no future-controller dependency graph is formed as an unpaid endpoint;
5. no temporal blocker-stability theorem is required.

The dependency and final-insertion alternatives of PP3apz and PP3ara apply only
when the complete-support or external marked-host interface is unavailable.

## 7. Revised moving-controller frontier

### Corollary PP3arh -- PROVED

The moving-controller architecture now has the exact split.

1. **Complete-support branch:** zero-cost punctured-star trades make strict nested
   progress and terminate.
2. **Robust branch:** direct allocation completes or produces another free
   super-target source star.
3. **External-host branch:** controller-disjoint helpers, distinguished endpoints,
   source-clean transitions, Hall/alternating/matching structure, or controller-
   preserving placement fails before the complete support theorem can be applied.
4. **Noncanonical branch:** the trade cannot be represented by the finite marked
   source and `A_2/B_3/B_4` insertion normal form.

Thus puncture reserve histories, future-controller dependencies, and
final-universe insertion cost are no longer internal frontiers of the canonical
complete-support branch.

The no-three-in-line conjecture remains unproved.
