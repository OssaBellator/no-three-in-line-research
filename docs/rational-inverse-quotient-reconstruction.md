# Canonical reconstruction of prime-field rational-inverse quotient labels

**Branch:** `research/rational-inverse-expansion`

RI6 retains abstract subgroup, coset, root, image and scale labels in addition to
physical rational-inverse occurrences.  In the prime-field route these labels are
not independent.  The multiplicative group is cyclic, a subgroup is determined by
its order, every quotient coset has a canonical exponent address, and the root,
image, companion and scale cosets are reconstructed from one physical factor.

This note removes only abstract naming entropy.  It does not install a component,
prove scale coherence, select a paid owner, or close recurrent physical-lift
collateral.

## Prime-field convention

Fix an odd prime `p`, put

\[
N=p-1,
\]

and fix the least primitive root `gamma` of `F_p^*`.  For each divisor `h | N`, set

\[
k=N/h,
\qquad
H_h=\langle\gamma^k\rangle.
\]

The quotient `F_p^*/H_h` has order `k`.

## RI5af -- subgroup order is a complete address -- PROVED

For every divisor `h | N`, `H_h` is the unique multiplicative subgroup of
`F_p^*` of order `h`, and every multiplicative subgroup is one of these groups.
Thus the canonical subgroup address is the integer `h`, with total stock

\[
\boxed{\tau(N)\le N.}
\]

### Proof

The multiplicative group of a finite prime field is cyclic of order `N`.  A cyclic
group has exactly one subgroup of each order dividing its order, generated here by
`gamma^(N/h)`.  There are `tau(N)` divisors, and trivially `tau(N)<=N`. QED.

This statement does not cover an unrelated abstract ambient group or a subgroup
without a declared prime-field realization.

## RI5ag -- canonical quotient-coset arithmetic -- PROVED

For `h | N` and `k=N/h`, every coset of `H_h` has one unique address

\[
\boxed{(h,j),\qquad 0\le j<k,}
\]

meaning `gamma^j H_h`.  In these addresses,

\[
(\gamma^iH_h)(\gamma^jH_h)=\gamma^{i+j}H_h,
\qquad
(\gamma^iH_h)^{-1}=\gamma^{-i}H_h,
\]

with exponents modulo `k`.  The complete subgroup--coset stock is

\[
\boxed{
C_{\rm cos}(N)
=
\sum_{h\mid N}\frac Nh
=
\sigma(N)
\le N\tau(N)
\le N^2.
}
\]

### Proof

The quotient is cyclic of order `k` generated by `gamma H_h`, so the listed cosets
are complete and unique and the arithmetic is exponent addition modulo `k`.
Summing the quotient orders gives `sigma(N)`.  Each summand is at most `N` and
there are `tau(N)` divisors. QED.

No algorithmic discrete-logarithm claim is needed.  The address is a mathematical
canonical dictionary; an implementation may instead retain the explicit coset or
its least field element.

## Physical rational-inverse factor

Let nonzero field elements `a,b,x,u,z` satisfy

\[
r=b/a\ne1,
\qquad
c=z/x\notin\{1,r\},
\qquad
g=u/x=F_r(c)=\frac{c(1-c)}{r-c}.
\]

Define the companion root

\[
c^\dagger=\tau_r(c)=\frac{r(c-1)}{c-r}.
\]

For a declared subgroup order `h`, let `R,A,B,C,S` be the quotient exponent
addresses of `r,c,c^dagger,g,x` respectively.

## RI5ah -- physical factors reconstruct root, image and scale cosets -- PROVED

The physical tuple `(a,b,x,u,z)` determines

\[
\boxed{r,c,g,c^\dagger}
\]

and, for every declared `h`, determines the quotient addresses `R,A,B,C,S`.
They obey

\[
\boxed{B=R+C-A\pmod{k},}
\]

and the physical partner and anchor cosets have addresses

\[
\boxed{uH_h=C+S,\qquad zH_h=A+S\pmod{k}.}
\]

Hence the two root cosets, rational image coset, companion coset and base-scale
coset are derived data once the physical occurrence and subgroup order are fixed.

### Proof

Field division gives `r=b/a`, `c=z/x` and `g=u/x`; the displayed rational formula
gives `c^dagger`.  Direct multiplication gives

\[
cc^\dagger=rg,
\]

so exponent addresses satisfy `A+B=R+C`, which is the first boxed relation.
The identities `u=gx` and `z=cx` give the second pair. QED.

The exclusions remove the zero and undefined rational-map cases.  Exceptional
normalizations retain their existing explicit conflict labels.

## RI5ai -- polynomial normalized and physical RI stocks -- PROVED

Retain one finite external role word `lambda` containing component type, channel
order, carry word, blocker occupancy and the already proved finite rank/closure
decorations.  Let the role-word stock be `L_RI`.  A normalized component uses at
most four source cosets.

For a subgroup of index `k`, encode a scale-free component by

\[
(h,m,U_1,\ldots,U_m,R,A,C,\lambda),
\qquad 1\le m\le4,
\]

reconstructing `B` from RI5ah.  A scale-localized component additionally retains
`S`.  Then

\[
\boxed{
N_{\rm RI}^{\rm norm}
\le
L_{\rm RI}\sum_{h\mid N}\sum_{m=1}^4(N/h)^{m+3}
\le4L_{\rm RI}N^8,
}
\]

and

\[
\boxed{
N_{\rm RI}^{\rm scale}
\le
L_{\rm RI}\sum_{h\mid N}\sum_{m=1}^4(N/h)^{m+4}
\le4L_{\rm RI}N^9.
}
\]

If one exact physical occurrence role is determined by `lambda` and the nonzero
five-tuple `(a,b,x,u,z)`, then including the subgroup order gives

\[
\boxed{
N_{\rm RI}^{\rm occ}
\le L_{\rm RI}\tau(N)N^5
\le L_{\rm RI}N^6.
}
\]

A weighted family of physical occurrences of total weight `W` therefore contains
one exact occurrence address of weight at least

\[
\boxed{W/N_{\rm RI}^{\rm occ}.}
\]

### Proof

For fixed `h`, every quotient coordinate has `k=N/h` choices.  A scale-free word
uses at most seven quotient coordinates: four source cosets and `R,A,C`; adding
`S` gives at most eight.  Sum over `m=1,...,4`, bound each power by `N^7` or
`N^8`, and use `tau(N)<=N`.  For a physical occurrence, choose `h`, the five
nonzero field elements and `lambda`; the validity equations only reduce the
ambient product.  Weighted pigeonhole gives the final claim. QED.

These are safe ambient stocks.  Distinct-source and RI compatibility constraints
make the true dictionaries smaller.

## RI5aj -- corrected abstract-label frontier -- PROVED UNDER THE PRIME-FIELD PHYSICAL CONTRACT

For canonical prime-field rational-inverse routes with a fixed primitive-root
convention:

1. subgroup identity is reconstructed from its order by RI5af;
2. quotient cosets and their arithmetic use RI5ag's canonical addresses;
3. root, image, companion and scale cosets are reconstructed from the physical
   occurrence and subgroup order by RI5ah;
4. normalized, scale-localized and physical occurrence dictionaries have RI5ai's
   polynomial stocks;
5. none of those derived labels is an independent state coordinate.

The following remain separate:

- the generator or selected set of source cosets;
- the assignment of exact physical occurrences to one component;
- scale-coherence and component-payability failures;
- owner, blocker and completion assignments;
- an external role dictionary without a polynomial `L_RI` bound;
- non-prime-field or unrelated abstract quotient groups;
- recurrence of one exact polynomially addressed component or occurrence.

### Proof

RI5af--RI5ai reconstruct every deleted field as a deterministic function of
retained physical data and the subgroup order.  The remaining fields are choices,
assignments or hypotheses not determined by those inputs. QED.

## Consequence for RI6

In the prime-minus-one regime `N=p-1`, abstract subgroup and coset names no longer
form an unbounded RI6 alphabet.  Scale-localized normalized components have stock
`O(L_RI N^9)` and exact physical occurrences have stock `O(L_RI N^6)`.  The live
RI6 obstruction is therefore physical selection, coherence, owner payment,
completion collateral and recurrence of exact addressed components, not unnamed
quotient arithmetic.
