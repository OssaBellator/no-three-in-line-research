# Every fixed slab allocation attempt completes or makes direct paid progress

The remaining frontier in the focused ledger is the initial controller-aware macro
allocation.  At a **fixed** source, pool family, label set and candidate-entry
universe, its numerical alternatives are already complete.

If an individual movement or refill label loses a fixed fraction of its controller
domain, PP3alc--PP3alh produce a target star or resource bank.  Otherwise all score
denominators have uniform linear margin.  The random two-sided local Ore theorem then
either completes the allocation or forces an `Omega(RT)` score numerator.  The four
possible numerator masses are converted by PP3ali--PP3alt and PP3aln--PP3alt.  Every
resulting credited structure is now spent directly by PP3auc--PP3aui.

Thus one fixed attempt has no unresolved arithmetic or endpoint-host branch.  Its
only outcomes are a completed patch or strict decrease of the current fixed-universe
potential.

## 1. Fixed attempt data

Fix:

1. one current saturated no-three source `S`;
2. the slab pools `E_1,...,E_M`;
3. the movement and refill label sets;
4. the controller-aware candidate-entry universe `V`; and
5. the dynamic potential `Psi_V`.

No pool, label or candidate entry is added during the attempt.  Candidate entries may
be deleted by selected-controller puncturing.

### Proposition PP3auq -- PROVED

Every source repair produced inside the fixed attempt acts in a nested candidate
universe `V' subseteq V`.  Entry deletion is favourable, and a direct support-free
credited trade has

```text
Psi_(V')(S')-Psi_V(S)<=-c(D)<0.
```

#### Proof

Selected-controller puncturing only deletes entries.  Entrywise deletion is
nonpositive by PP3aqu--PP3ara.  The direct trade inequality is PP3aud--PP3aue. ∎

## 2. Individual label-margin alternative

Let `delta>0` be the fixed margin used in the controller-aware compatibility graphs.

### Proposition PP3aur -- PROVED / CONDITIONAL EXISTING CONVERSION INTERFACES

If some movement or refill label has at least `delta R` unsafe controller entries,
then the fixed attempt yields:

1. a degree-`W` source/blocker star; or
2. an `Omega(W)` fully resource-disjoint credited bank.

Either structure gives strict direct paid progress by PP3auf or PP3aue.

#### Proof

Use PP3alc--PP3alh and its transposed version, then apply PP3auc--PP3aui. ∎

Hence failure of direct paid progress implies the uniform denominator bounds required
by PP3alu--PP3alz.

## 3. Random two-sided score alternative

Assume all individual label margins survive.  Let `rho_i(A)` and `chi_i(B)` be the
movement and refill complementary scores and put

```text
h=2T sqrt(log(4MT)/W)=o(T).
```

### Theorem PP3aus -- PROVED / CONDITIONAL RANDOM TWO-SIDED ALLOCATION INTERFACE

Exactly one of the following occurs.

1. Every relevant pair satisfies

   ```text
   rho_i(A)+chi_i(B)<=T-h,
   ```

   and the random two-sided theorem completes the balanced ownership and global label
   allocation.

2. Some pair violates the inequality, and one of

   ```text
   A_i, B_i, U_i(A), V_i(B)
   ```

   has mass at least

   ```text
   delta R(T-h)/4=Omega(RT).
   ```

#### Proof

This is PP3alu--PP3alz after the denominator margin supplied by the negation of
PP3aur. ∎

Score truncation at `T` is included in the same numerator certificate.

## 4. Conversion of every large numerator

### Proposition PP3aut -- PROVED / CONDITIONAL EXISTING CONVERSION INTERFACES

Every mass alternative in PP3aus yields a target-order credited structure.

1. `U_i(A)` or `V_i(B)` gives a fixed-label anchor row/column structure converted by
   PP3ali--PP3alm or PP3aln--PP3alt.
2. `A_i` or `B_i` gives a fixed-macro movement/refill defect core converted by
   PP3aln--PP3alt and the fixed-macro resource extraction.
3. Any dense blocker or anchor subbranch gives one of the source stars, endpoint
   banks, anchor banks or full resource matchings already covered by PP3aug.

Every resulting structure either pays directly or converts once more to a structure
that pays directly.

#### Proof

Apply the cited mass-conversion theorems and then PP3aug. ∎

No score-numerator branch remains as an unspent obstruction.

## 5. Fixed-attempt completion-or-progress theorem

### Theorem PP3auu -- PROVED / CONDITIONAL ESTABLISHED SLAB INTERFACES

For one fixed controller-aware slab allocation attempt, assume the standard patch-only
and ordinary source-anchor completion energies are within their established `o(1)`
budgets.  Then exactly one of the following occurs.

1. The balanced random two-sided allocation succeeds and the simultaneous saturated
   no-three macro patch is installed.
2. A source-admissible saturation-preserving direct repair strictly decreases the
   nested fixed-universe potential.

#### Proof

If an individual margin fails, use PP3aur.  Otherwise apply PP3aus.  Its successful
branch installs the patch by PP3ho and the slab completion theorem.  Its failure
branch is converted and paid by PP3aut and PP3auq. ∎

All local endpoint, controller, coordinate and reference requirements of the repair
branch have already been discharged by PP3ath--PP3aup.

## 6. What remains between attempts

### Corollary PP3auv -- PROVED

The initial macro-allocation frontier is no longer a single-attempt theorem.  It is
the following exact restart problem:

> After a strict paid repair of the source, compare the next allocation attempt with
> the previous fixed candidate universe strongly enough to prevent pool/label restart
> from replenishing the potential that was just removed.

Sufficient closures would include any one of:

1. retaining the same pools and labels for all repairs until allocation succeeds;
2. a universal candidate-entry potential dominating every permitted restart;
3. a bounded number of restarts with aggregate puncture and pool loss `o(R)`; or
4. a monotone restart rule whose newly introduced entries have zero initial blocker
   mass.

The local allocation arithmetic, score truncation, endpoint host and direct payment
are not part of this remaining problem.

## 7. Revised final frontier

### Corollary PP3auw -- PROVED

Within the slab-optimal controller-aware architecture, the sole concentrated frontier
is **restart comparability across successive fixed allocation attempts**.

A proof of a monotone or universally dominated restart rule would combine PP3auu with
the nonnegative integer potential to force eventual macro-patch completion.  Without
such a rule, strict progress in one fixed universe does not by itself exclude a jump
when a new pool or label universe is introduced.

Genuinely different architectures remain outside this focused chain.

The no-three-in-line conjecture remains unproved.
