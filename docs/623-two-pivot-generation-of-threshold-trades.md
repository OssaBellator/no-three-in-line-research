# Two-pivot generation of threshold three-cycle trades

`docs/617` classified the eight nearest legal degree-four threshold matrices as
unit alternating three-cycle trades.  This chapter connects those trades to the
conservative residual-flow operations underlying `docs/509`.

## 1. Conservative `2 x 2` pivots

A unit pivot on rows `r_1,r_2` and columns `c_1,c_2` subtracts one unit from one
diagonal of the rectangle and adds one unit to the other diagonal.  It is allowed
when no entry becomes negative.  Every pivot preserves all row and column sums.

### Theorem PP3cwj — PROVED / TWO-PIVOT REACHABILITY

Every one of the eight nearest legal degree-four matrices is reachable from the
stored source matrix by exactly two allowed conservative `2 x 2` pivots.

No target is reachable by one pivot.

#### Proof

The checker regenerates all eighteen legal permutation layers and all 4,475
distinct legal four-layer matrices.  It identifies the eight distance-six
targets, enumerates every allowed first pivot from the source and every allowed
second pivot, and compares the final matrices exactly. ∎

## 2. Shortest-path multiplicity

### Theorem PP3cwk — PROVED / SIX RESIDUAL PATHS PER TARGET

Each of the eight targets has exactly six ordered shortest two-pivot paths from
the source matrix.  Every intermediate matrix is nonnegative and has row and
column margins four.

#### Proof

Direct two-step enumeration gives six paths for every target.  Entrywise and
margin checks are applied after each pivot. ∎

## 3. Source-generation consequence

### Theorem PP3cwl — PROVED / MATRIX-LEVEL SOURCE PATH

The alternating three-cycle trades are generated by the same conservative
bipartite residual-cycle mechanism used in the proof of `PP3cjf`.  Therefore the
threshold candidate no longer needs a new margin-changing fifth slot or a new
matrix algebra: it needs a geometric realization of two standard conservative
residual pivots.

#### Proof

A `2 x 2` pivot is an integral circulation on one bipartite four-cycle.  The two
stored pivots compose to each target while preserving margins at every stage. ∎

## Remaining geometric obligation

The pivots are repository-native source-matrix operations, but no theorem maps a
pivot rectangle to a legal prime-patching edit on actual source cells.  This
completes the candidate matrix-level source path without promoting the geometric
threshold row.
