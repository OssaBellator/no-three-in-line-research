# Paired-secant source calls have no raw host leaf

The dense source-support row is realized through fixed-template secant descent,
resource-disjoint paired four-state switches, and a fixed-centre paired-switch star with
bounded fillers.  Its earlier endpoint statements still retain conditional Hall,
alternating, non-superregular, role-host, or locally impossible outputs.

At the actual call sites these are bounded complete-support calls.  The marked scale is
at most `W`, quadratic helper supply is automatic, selected-controller and role-domain
losses are linear, and every positive local event has nonempty ordinary-helper support
of rank at most three.  The raw second-host theorem PP3bai--PP3ban therefore supersedes
the old host alternatives.

In the paired-switch bank, the remaining two-inserted-cell source mass vanishes after
chromatic amplification and hard unary source support is current `Xi_old` credit.  In
the fixed-centre filler call, a dense source branch is already a canonical credited
source star, pencil, or bank and is paid by PP3auf--PP3aug.

This chapter removes raw source-host feasibility from the dense source row.  It does not
prove the global prime-minus-one seed theorem.

## 1. Exact call-site hypotheses

Consider either:

1. one resource-disjoint bank of paired four-state secant switches from
   PP3azk--PP3azo; or
2. one fixed-centre secant record together with its tentative-block and witness-block
   filler roles from PP3azp.

Let `r` be the number of marked roles in the current bounded call.

### Proposition PP3bbz -- PROVED

At both call types:

```text
1 <= r <= W,
```

the permanent matching block and source layer are explicit, the available ordinary
helper reservoir has size at least `r^2`, every role loses only `O(r)` candidates, and
the complete positive-event support table has ordinary-helper rank at most three.

#### Proof

The paired-switch alphabet has bounded cell support and source-event rank at most three
by PP3azn.  The filler call imposes only bounded endpoint, source, current-potential, and
puncture conditions by PP3azp.  The complete second-host scale, helper, puncture, and
role-domain audit is PP3baa--PP3bah; quadratic coordinate supply is PP3bao--PP3bap.
Every positive local event is included by the complete support theorem and
PP3bai. ∎

## 2. Raw trichotomy at a paired source call

## 4. Paired-switch source mass is not terminal

### Proposition PP3bcc -- PROVED

For a resource-disjoint paired-switch bank at the adaptive secondary scale:

1. anchored-pair, transition, and inserted-triple source terms have normalized total
   `o(1)` by PP3azf;

source-host obstruction in the paired-switch bank.  It yields a source-valid joint

#### Proof

The paired switch has fixed alphabet and rank-at-most-three source events by PP3azn.
Apply PP3azf to the two-inserted-cell classes and PP3aze to hard unary support.  These
exhaust the source-event types in the complete table. ∎

This removes item 4 of PP3azo as an independent host alternative.

## 6. Acyclic dense-source endpoint

### Theorem PP3bce -- PROVED / CONDITIONAL ESTABLISHED CURRENT PAYMENT

A complete secant-shadow source branch has exactly one of the following outcomes.

1. Fixed-template descent selects a secant-clean source-valid completion.
2. A paired-switch bank selects a source-valid joint state and spends the original
   designated credit.
3. Hard unary or dense current support enters a named current conversion and yields
   strict payment or direct completion.
4. A fixed-centre dense source structure is paid directly as a credited source
   star/bank.

No branch terminates in raw Hall, alternating, non-superregular, role-host, locally
impossible, or cumulative untyped source-host structure.

#### Proof

Use PP3ayj--PP3aym for the clean-arc route and PP3ayp--PP3azr for the secant-cover
conversion.  Apply PP3bca at every bounded completion call, route dense current by
PP3bcb, paired-bank source mass by PP3bcc, and fixed-centre source support by PP3bcd. ∎

### Corollary PP3bcf -- PROVED

The dense source row is now acyclic relative to the current row:

```text
dense source
 -> source-valid joint completion
 or named current payment/direct completion.
```

It cannot regenerate a raw source-host feasibility problem.  The remaining local
frontier is the named unary/source/base-domain current concentration set of PP3bby.
The separate global frontier is the prime-minus-one seed theorem.

The no-three-in-line conjecture remains unproved.
