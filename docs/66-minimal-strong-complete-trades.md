# Minimal support of strong-complete trades

PX98 gives a nontrivial strong-complete trade on four rows.  This chapter proves
that four is the minimum possible support over every prime field of odd
characteristic greater than three.

Let `f,g:F_p -> F_p` be strong complete mappings.  Suppose they agree outside a
set

\[
X=\{x_1,\ldots,x_k\}.
\]

Because both maps are permutations and agree outside `X`, their image multisets
on `X` coincide.  The same is true for the difference and sum colour multisets:

\[
\{f(x_i)\}_i=\{g(x_i)\}_i,
\]

\[
\{x_i-f(x_i)\}_i=\{x_i-g(x_i)\}_i,
\]

\[
\{x_i+f(x_i)\}_i=\{x_i+g(x_i)\}_i.
\]

## Theorem PX102 -- PROVED

For every prime `p>3`, two distinct strong complete mappings differ on at least
four rows.

Consequently the PX98 four-row trade has minimum possible support.

### Proof

Put

\[
y_i=f(x_i),
\qquad
z_i=g(x_i).
\]

The image multisets agree, so

\[
\sum_i y_i^m=\sum_i z_i^m
\]

for every nonnegative integer `m`.

Equality of the sum-colour and difference-colour multisets gives equality of
their second power sums.  Subtracting

\[
\sum_i(x_i-y_i)^2
=
\sum_i(x_i-z_i)^2
\]

from

\[
\sum_i(x_i+y_i)^2
=
\sum_i(x_i+z_i)^2
\]

and using `p` odd yields

\[
\sum_i x_i y_i
=
\sum_i x_i z_i.
\]

Likewise, equality of the third power sums and the identity

\[
(x+y)^3-(x-y)^3=6x^2y+2y^3
\]

give, because `p>3` and the image cubes have equal sums,

\[
\sum_i x_i^2y_i
=
\sum_i x_i^2z_i.
\]

If `k=1`, equality of image multisets already gives `y_1=z_1`.
If `k=2`, the equations

\[
\sum_i(y_i-z_i)=0,
\qquad
\sum_i x_i(y_i-z_i)=0
\]

have coefficient matrix

\[
\begin{pmatrix}
1&1\\
x_1&x_2
\end{pmatrix},
\]

whose determinant is nonzero because the rows are distinct.  Hence `y_i=z_i`
for both indices.

If `k=3`, use also the quadratic moment.  The coefficient matrix is the
Vandermonde matrix

\[
\begin{pmatrix}
1&1&1\\
x_1&x_2&x_3\\
x_1^2&x_2^2&x_3^2
\end{pmatrix},
\]

which is invertible for three distinct rows.  Again `y_i=z_i` for every `i`.

Therefore a nontrivial trade cannot have support one, two, or three.  PX98
supplies support four. \(\square\)

## Corollary PX102a -- PROVED

Every edge of the strong-complete switching graph generated by arbitrary exact
support-minimal trades is a PX98-sized four-row exchange.  Other four-row trade
shapes may exist, but no smaller switching can improve the mixing problem from
PX100.

