# Exact radius-three selector layers for the completed side-seven classes

PX540 and PX545 prove that the `(3,2,2)` and `(4,3)` classes contain no
coordinate-embeddable selector at alternating-cycle distance two from their
certified centres.  The next selector-only task is therefore exact
breadth-first generation of distance three.

## 1. Exact third layer

Let `L_0={F_0}` be the centre, and let `L_1,L_2` be the exact layers from
PX514 and PX519.  Define

\[
L_3=
\left(\bigcup_{F\in L_2}N(F)\right)
\setminus(L_0\cup L_1\cup L_2),
\]

where `N(F)` is the set of simple alternating-cycle neighbours.

### Theorem PX546 -- PROVED

The displayed set is exactly the selector-cycle distance-three layer.

### Proof

Every generated selector is reached by a three-edge path from the centre and is
not in an earlier layer, hence has distance exactly three.  Conversely every
distance-three selector has a distance-two predecessor on a shortest path and
therefore appears in the union. \(\square\)
