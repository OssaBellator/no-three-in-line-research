# Open bottlenecks and research roadmap

## 1. Why inherited context is necessary

Exact normalized four-endpoint traps exist. A proof based only on the current
small-board type and triple potential cannot establish global descent. Both the
prime-field and prime-power routes require inherited parent context, reserve,
ancestry, or envelope expansion.

## 2. Closed composite prime-power reduction

The live branch now has the following proved chain.

1. **CMR123--CMR218:** global-baseline contraction, inherited parent banks, Hall
   walls, peeling, essentiality, and exchange ancestry.
2. **CMR219--CMR359:** primitive-height localization, thin-blocker signatures,
   line-clean paid-pair banks, walls, secant stars, heavy prefix cells, and full
   tokens.
3. **CMR360--CMR409:** tunable token batching, exact completion of intermediate
   bands, harmonic-packet construction, and exact prefix/packet reset costs.
4. **CMR410--CMR428:** selected-state cycle erasure, entering-edge recreation
   support, deletion-or-forced ancestry, essentiality persistence, and
   polynomial packet completion-or-terminal-certificate scheduling.
5. **CMR429--CMR438:** final essential core of size at most `t`, polynomial
   edge-set ancestry, exact exchange corridors, Dilworth batch-cycle covers, and
   at most `t` historical exchange cycles.
6. **CMR439--CMR447:** common-epoch sparse rollback, minimum rollback forced-core
   factorization, exact token incidence, and harmonic packet recreation cost.
7. **CMR448--CMR471:** minimum-cost tight faces, optimum-sensitive potentials,
   SCC products, sparse balanced level skeletons, right-column polarization, and
   the mixed-colour cycle endpoint.
8. **CMR472--CMR491:** mixed-cycle packing/concentration, directed Menger theta
   fans and cuts, private theta-route payment, rooted secant stars, and fixed
   rank-two cylinders.
9. **CMR492--CMR501:** universal compatible-pair line-clean derangement
   cylinders and cheap-restoration versus forced-factorization availability.

The branch is no longer missing packet construction, packet termination,
polynomial ancestry, temporal lifting, mixed-cycle normalization, a compatible-
pair line-clean bank, or an availability price.

## 3. Current principal bottleneck: cheap line-clean selection

Fix a compatible paid pair and its line-clean universe `U_L`. In a restricted
current residual host `G_L`, define

\[
\kappa_L(G)
=
\min\{|M\setminus E(G_L)|:M\in\operatorname{PM}(U_L)\}.
\]

CMR497--CMR501 prove

\[
0\le\kappa_L(G)\le m-2.
\]

For every threshold `q`, either:

1. fewer than `q` residual edges are restored, with exact labelled incidence
   below
   \[
   (p+1)(h-1)q,
   \]
   and recreated conflicts supported by those restored edges; or
2. a forced restoration matching of size at least `q` factors the residual
   problem to side at most `m-2-q`.

The unresolved theorem is entirely inside the cheap branch.

### Required selection theorem

Among the line-clean completions made available by a small restoration
footprint, prove at least one of:

- one completion strictly lowers inherited target load or global potential;
- every nonimproving completion consumes protected reserve;
- frozen-bank averaging forces a heavy prefix cell, primitive-height cluster,
  quotient/carry signature, or repeated full token;
- the completion family factors into smaller hosts;
- or the canonical envelope expands.

CMR334 already provides the correct rank-zero/rank-one frozen-bank average. The
next step is to combine it with CMR500's restoration support and token price.

## 4. Secondary bottlenecks

### Sparse mixed-interface recursion

CMR476 deletes fewer than `q` matching pairs when cyclic colour-boundary arcs are
sparse, leaving a colour-separated residual host. A complete termination theorem
must charge that deletion to a strictly decreasing side length, reserve stock,
or envelope depth.

### Low-height carry integration

Generic codegree estimates remain weak at low primitive height. Existing width-
two, width-three, quotient, carry-cell, and line-energy alternatives must be fed
into the same line-clean selection or envelope budget rather than developed as
another isolated extraction chain.

### Prime-field transfer

The complementary-hyperbola route still needs the inherited-envelope,
minimum-cost, and exact-covering endpoint now available on the prime-power route.

### Arbitrary side lengths

Even a complete prime-power theorem would not cover every `n`. Remaining options
include additional balanced prime factorizations, controlled products, CRT
absorption, or patching between nearby admissible sizes.

## 5. Open lemmas in recommended order

1. **Cheap-cylinder frozen average.** Combine CMR334 and CMR500 to bound the
   average nonrooted collateral over a cheaply restored line-clean cylinder.
2. **Restored-edge heavy/dispersion split.** Show that restored-edge-supported
   collateral either concentrates on a matching vertex/prefix/carry signature or
   disperses enough to pay private full-token incidence.
3. **Net target-load descent.** Deduce one line-clean completion which destroys
   more inherited load than it recreates.
4. **Sparse-interface measure.** Formalize a monotone payment for CMR476 boundary
   deletion.
5. **Low-height carry absorption.** Integrate the remaining thin signatures.
6. **Prime-field transfer.** Rebuild the same endpoint for prime fields.
7. **Balanced-family and CRT assembly.** Cover arbitrary side lengths.

## 6. Computational priorities

- Enumerate cheaply restored line-clean cylinders for exact terminal hosts.
- Measure rank-zero/rank-one collateral by restored edge, full token, primitive
  height, quotient, and carry signature.
- Test whether nonimproving cylinders reproduce CMR390 walls/secant stars or
  CMR342 heavy-prefix cells.
- Search for a monotone sparse-interface measure compatible with the exact token-
  restoring cycles of CMR350.
- Continue balanced-factorization and finite side-length searches.

## 7. Current proved endpoint

Through CMR501 the following are closed at their stated scales:

- exact harmonic-packet completion and reset accounting;
- selected-state and packet recurrence payment;
- essential-core ancestry and exchange-corridor compression;
- sparse common-epoch rollback and forced-core factorization;
- minimum-cost tight-face, SCC, level, and colour normalization;
- mixed-cycle packing, concentration, theta-fan, and private-edge payment;
- rooted secant-star and fixed rank-two cylinder identification;
- universal compatible-pair line-clean derangement banks;
- line-clean current-host availability, token price, and expensive-case
  factorization.

There is still no complete proof of the no-three-in-line conjecture.
