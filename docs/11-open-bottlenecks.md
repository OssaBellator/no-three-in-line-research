## 1. Current proof status

The no-three-in-line conjecture remains open. The branch now has exact
structural reductions, response laws, line-clean and return certificate forms,

formulas, matching-level thin denominator censuses, a geometric-fibre correction,

> finite response, finite resource use and structural descent are not by
> themselves potential improvement.

A completion must exhibit a lower-potential response or an exact weighted
inequality guaranteeing one.

## 6. Geometric-fibre correction

The normalization `(O,e)=(I_d,(0,1))` and the residual `S_{d-2}` action are exact
for matching counts and prescription probabilities. They are not automatically
exact for Euclidean geometry.

For every `d>=3`, the only residual simultaneous coordinate permutation preserving
all standard-grid collinear triples is the identity. Therefore the 45 side-four
and 124 side-five canonical hosts are matching denominator classes, not final
geometric rows.

For each matching-level fibre, the final computation must:

1. expand every exact geometric embedding or line-incidence signature;
2. quotient only by verified automorphisms of the complete geometric and
   provenance signature; or
3. take an explicit componentwise maximum over the fibre.

One arbitrary matching representative is not an honest geometric quotient.

## 7. Exact marginal and rook compiler

Define geometric prescription coefficients

\[
a_1(x)=\sum_{\ell\ni x}C(|B\cap\ell|,2),
\]

\[
a_2(\{x,y\})=|B\cap\ell(x,y)|,
\]

and let `a_3(P)` indicate a collinear response triple.

For every response law,

\[
\mathbb E[\Psi(B\cup Q)-\Psi(B)]
=
\sum_xa_1(x)p(x)
+
\sum_{|P|=2}a_2(P)p(P)
+
\sum_{|P|=3}a_3(P)p(P).
\]

The rank-one term is an ordinary assignment score and admits superlevel or
source/target-cover dual certificates. Rank-two and rank-three are exact joint
marginals.

`z(P)=N_{d-|P|}(F/P)`, the exact numerator is

\[
A_{\mathrm{line}}
=
\sum_xa_1(x)z(x)
+
\sum_{|P|=2}a_2(P)z(P)
+
\sum_{|P|=3}a_3(P)z(P).
\]

The direct strict certificate is

\[
A_{\mathrm{line}}<ZD.
\]

This calculation is performed separately in every exact geometric fibre.
