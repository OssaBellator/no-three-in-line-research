# Open bottlenecks and research roadmap

## Bottleneck 1: terminal repair needs ancestry

Exact four-endpoint traps exist. CMR143--CMR144 give a potential-one terminal
two-cycle at `N=4` even though a separate potential-zero saturated state exists.
Therefore no theorem depending only on normalized four-board type and current
triple potential can prove global descent.

The prime-field and prime-power routes both require a larger inherited move,
not another purely local four-cell rule.

## Bottleneck 2: inherited parent escape

The composite branch now has the following complete reduction.

1. CMR123--CMR137 retain a fixed global baseline and contract every positive
   target load to one four-endpoint target.
2. CMR145--CMR152 prove exact complete-parent escapes at the prime-five and
   prime-seven roots.
3. CMR153--CMR180 construct old-cell-clean joint parents, prove all split-rank
   collateral bounds, and establish persistent nonroot disjoint row fibres.
4. CMR172--CMR175 attach every branch to a canonical prefix envelope whose row
   sets are invariant and whose depth decreases at most `k` times.
5. CMR181--CMR195 classify inherited terminal covers, prove the `9/11` parent
   cap, generate a `2/11` batch deficit, and pay every crossing defect by strict
   envelope expansion.
6. CMR196--CMR218 force linear real-line support, extract Hall walls, peel to a
   half-degree host, prove every half-degree edge is deletable, and attach an
   acyclic alternating-exchange ancestry to later forced cells.
7. CMR219--CMR222 apply the matching-space local lemma. A frozen parent of size
   `t>=48` either exposes an executable rank-one/rank-two bank or has one parent
   row or column incident with more than
   \[
   \frac{t(t-1)(t-2)}{144}
   \]
   candidate-only triples. One dyadic primitive-height band receives at least a
   `1/ceil(log_2 t)` fraction of this population.
8. CMR223--CMR226 prove that a band
   \[
   H\le\max(|u|,|v|)<2H
   \]
   has conflict degree below `3t^2` and pair codegree below `t/H`. When
   `H>=t^epsilon`, the standard bounded-conflict hypotheses hold, and the
   published conflict-free matching theorem gives an almost-perfect
   band-avoiding derangement matching.

The inherited escape problem is therefore split into two exact tasks.

### High primitive heights

Construct a reserve/absorption model which upgrades CMR226 from an
almost-perfect matching to a full parent permutation while preserving all
already protected bands.

The natural duplicated-row model uses:

- source columns as the class to cover exactly;
- main and reserve copies of parent rows;
- mixed conflicts forbidding repeated use of one original row;
- all candidate triples containing main or reserve edges.

The immediate theorem is to verify the mixed degree and codegree conditions of
the Joos--Mubayi--Smith conflict-free matching-and-covering theorem in this
model.

### Low primitive heights

For bands `H<t^epsilon`, the generic pair-codegree hypothesis is too weak.
These bands must be charged using the prime-power structure:

- first-separation depth;
- quotient modular syndrome;
- primitive direction and carry cells;
- inherited envelope row fibres;
- or a reverse-scale target budget.

This is the remaining algebraic carry problem.

## Bottleneck 3: coarse-to-fine recreation

Fine-to-coarse prefix processing preserves every unprocessed coarser quotient
charge. A later coarse repair can still recreate fine stars. A complete proof
needs a lexicographic account in which recreated fine mass is paid by at least
one of:

1. the coarse target load destroyed by the move;
2. strict envelope-depth expansion;
3. a new primitive line/carry signature;
4. the reserve edges used in exact high-band completion.

## Bottleneck 4: all side lengths

The prime-power programme now supplies:

- nonlinear full channels at every odd prime power;
- disjoint saturated companions;
- balanced recursive banks for every `p=1 mod 4`;
- a non-reciprocal balanced bank at every power of seven;
- quadratic-order syndrome and prefix-collateral bounds;
- executable prefix, child-pencil, terminal, and parent banks;
- an almost-perfect conflict-free endpoint for every high dyadic band.

Arbitrary `n` still requires a separate coverage mechanism: further balanced
prime factorizations, controlled products, CRT absorption, or patching between
nearby admissible sizes.

## Current proved endpoint

The following broad pieces are closed:

1. generic recursive first-separation summation;
2. binary-star localization and complete prefix rematching;
3. quotient charging and universal higher-rank prefix collateral;
4. recursive-compatible child repair and child-core cancellation;
5. vertical-pencil concentration and alternating extraction;
6. global-baseline and target-load contraction;
7. exact root trap/escape censuses at `p=5,7`;
8. closure-envelope ancestry and parent-cover lifting;
9. Hall-wall peeling, half-degree flexibility, and exchange ancestry;
10. matching-space local-load concentration;
11. dyadic band conflict degree/codegree verification;
12. published almost-perfect conflict-free matching for high bands.

## Open lemmas in recommended order

1. **Exact high-band reserve completion.** Verify the duplicated-row mixed
   conflict system and apply a P-perfect conflict-free covering theorem.
2. **Low-band carry absorption.** Replace the raw `t/H` pair-codegree by a
   first-separation/carry bound when `H<t^epsilon`.
3. **Reverse-scale recreation budget.** Integrate the exact high/low band
   repairs with coarse-to-fine prefix processing.
4. **Prime-field transfer.** Rebuild the reserve-completion endpoint for
   complementary hyperbola carry cycles.
5. **Further balanced prime families.** Extend the prime-seven grid
   factorization to `p=11,19,...`, or prove structural obstructions.
6. **Square-root divisor boundary.** Remove or sum the nearly singular terms in
   CMR61 and CMR64.
7. **CRT and product assembly.** Control mixed projections and zero-divisor
   slope carries.
8. **Finite and arbitrary-size coverage.** Continue exact searches at `N=14`
   and develop a patching theorem.

## Computational priorities

- Build the duplicated-row reserve hypergraph and enumerate its mixed conflict
  degrees on small parent boards.
- Test exact completion after a high-band conflict-free almost-perfect matching.
- Record low-band pair-codegrees by first-separation and carry signature.
- Measure fine-star recreation under one coarse joint-parent move.
- Search for non-reciprocal balanced grid factorizations at the next
  `p=3 mod 4` primes.
- Continue the `N=14` and joint digital searches.

There is still no complete proof of the no-three-in-line conjecture.
