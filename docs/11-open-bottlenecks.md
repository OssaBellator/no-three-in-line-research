# Open bottlenecks and research roadmap

## 1. Current proof status

The no-three-in-line conjecture remains open.  The branch has exact structural,
matching-bank, inherited-coordinate, displacement and temporal reductions, but
no theorem yet proves that every positive minimum of the real-triple potential
becomes zero.

The decisive honesty condition is unchanged:

> finite response, finite resource use and structural descent are not by
> themselves potential improvement.

A completion must exhibit a lower-potential response or an exact weighted
inequality guaranteeing one.

## 2. Closed structural components

The selected execution has finite canonical forms for inherited response banks,
Hall walls, unit-wall products, rollback, exchange-SCC and protected/free
products, selected routing, strict children, fixed-interface lifting,
restoration ancestry, loaded-line/star banks and small-side factors.

Last-entering ownership makes the complete offspring matrix block upper
triangular:

\[
\rho(A)=\max_i\rho(A_{ii}).
\]

Only genuinely recurrent same-owner diagonal blocks need numerical
certificates.

Strict internal scaling, earlier-depth transfer, first use of a private edge,
first use of an absolute token and ordinary structural exits form an acyclic
transfer graph.  Once recurrent cores are certified, constructive rational
scaling absorbs all finite off-diagonal collateral.

## 3. Exact response and packed-displacement law

For response side `d`, opposite matching `O` and target `e`, use

\[
H_e=K_{d,d}\setminus(O\cup\{e\}).
\]

Every compatible prescription has one finite rook class and every candidate has
one canonical owner before sampling.  Expected collateral is one exact
bipartite assignment cost with a rational dual.

Inside envelope `p^k` with coordinate span `W_omega`, a positive-minimum
fractional obstruction contains one exact-displacement class of mass at least

\[
M_0=
\frac{d-2}
{6k(p+1)B_\omega D_p(H)S_{\omega,p}(s,H)}.
\]

That bank routes to strict scaling, earlier depth, an absolute token, or a
root-residue channel.  Its translation support contains a large endpoint-
disjoint private subbank.  Loaded-owner and private translated currencies have
disjoint canonical owner support.

## 4. Closed extension-free line coordinate

CMR1510--CMR1517 show that every Hall-cut blocker of `H_e` has more edges than a
partial matching can place in the same cut rectangle.  Hence

\[
\operatorname{PM}(H_e\setminus X)\ne\varnothing
\]

for every partial matching `X` of allowed response edges.

Every nonaxis real line is a partial matching.  The complete allowed response
trace of a loaded line may therefore be forbidden while the response continues
to avoid both `O` and `e`.  The resulting state has no response-layer cell on
that line and creates no new line-local collateral.

For a CMR1461 owner-labelled loaded line, the same-owner, same-line offspring
coordinate has a policy row coefficient zero.  The remaining task is to bound
the off-line collateral created by that line-clean response.

## 5. Atomic repeated-token quotient

For one nonroot full token in an envelope `t=p^h`, repeated heavy episodes first
pay finite token-edge stock or identify one recurrent central edge.  Once that
edge is fixed, the pair and trace signature stocks are only

\[
(t-1)^2
\]

and

\[
2(t-1).
\]

Choose desired recurrence multiplicity `q` and return threshold `R`, and set

\[
\lambda=2Rq(t-1)^2.
\]

CMR1518--CMR1525 give an explicit five-way alternative:

1. a finite episode bound;
2. at least `R` edge reintroductions, with exact full-token incidence
   `R(p+1)(h-1)`;
3. adaptive absorption of the persistent edge;
4. at least `q` occurrences of one jointly persistent paid-pair selector with
   surcharge two;
5. at least `q` occurrences of one fixed trace-line or trace-incidence slot.

Thus repeated-token/reused-support recurrence has only three atomic numerical
rows:

- edge return;
- persistent paid-pair selector;
- fixed trace incidence.

Fresh stock and successful absorption are not independent recurrent classes.

## 6. Genuine recurrent rows

After all closed reductions, the host-uniform diagonal quotient still needs
coefficients for:

1. **Line-clean off-line row.**  Collateral away from the deleted loaded line.
2. **Return row.**  New credits supported by one labelled edge return.
3. **Persistent selector row.**  The CMR531 fixed paid-pair selector with
   deterministic restoration surcharge two.
4. **Trace row.**  One fixed real trace line or row/column incidence slot.
5. **Root/fixed-interface row.**  Recurrence of one source residue, quotient
   carry and lifted low-rank prescription.
6. **Thin row.**  Small scattered residual factors not covered by nonroot depth
   transfer.

The broad “loaded line” and “repeated token” labels should no longer appear as
single primitive rows.

## 7. Recommended next lemmas

1. **Off-line line-clean envelope.**  Use exact rook probabilities after deleting
   one complete line trace to bound all remaining owner/profile classes.
2. **Return coefficient.**  Combine exact entering-edge recreation support with
   `(p+1)(h-1)` labelled incidence per returned edge.
3. **Selector coefficient.**  Evaluate
   \[
   A_L+\frac2Q+\frac{|B_L|}{Q(n-1)}
   \]
   using the inherited-coordinate owner envelope and choose `Q` optimally.
4. **Trace coefficient.**  Convert one fixed trace incidence into a loaded-line,
   secant-star, prefix/carry, or fixed-interface row without losing provenance.
5. **Root/fixed-interface coefficient.**  Enumerate exact offspring by partner
   type, source residue, quotient carry and local rank.
6. **Core matrix.**  Assemble the surviving rows into a finite rational upper
   quotient and search for an integer certificate `Av<v`.
7. **Prime-field, thin and CRT rows.**  Evaluate the remaining base rows and glue
   them through owner triangularity while retaining collision/local-line labels.

## 8. Computational priorities

The next finite calculations should target coefficients rather than additional
structural alternatives.

- Enumerate extension-free line-clean rows after deleting every allowed cell on
  one nonaxis line.
- Enumerate return-supported offspring with exact last-entering owner labels.
- Evaluate the CMR531 selector bound over exact residual rook classes and
  restoration thresholds.
- Enumerate fixed trace-line rows with row/column provenance retained.
- Enumerate recurrent root/fixed-interface rows and thin scattered factors.
- Search rational weights, clear denominators and verify strict integer row
  inequalities independently.

## 9. Current proved endpoint

Through **CMR1525**:

- exact response probabilities and owner weights are known;
- inherited-coordinate capacity is valid for scattered residual factors;
- global packed displacement banks have quantitative lower bounds;
- strict depth transfers and fresh resources are off-diagonal;
- depth-zero banks split into deterministic root child channels;
- packed and loaded-owner currencies are owner-disjoint;
- arbitrary partial matchings, including complete nonaxis line traces, can be
  deleted from the extension-free host;
- repeated-token histories compress to return, persistent-selector or fixed-
  trace atomic rows.

There is still no complete proof.  The next genuine advance is an exact
coefficient bound for one of the surviving atomic rows, followed by a verified
host-uniform recurrent-core certificate.
