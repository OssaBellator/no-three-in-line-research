# Geometric-cleaning collision-segment localization

This note refines GC2kk--GC2ko on the complete physical/remedy/height/cause network. It turns each paired cause-path collision into one canonical contiguous cleaning-capacity segment.

## Contract

Fix the canonical irreducible cause core, its canonical split, the canonical cycle-cancelled integral unit paths paying both sides, and one named positive-pressure cut arc. A collision witness is one unit cause path from each side paired at one occupied capacity slot. Retain every cause, donor, remedy, height, physical-source, compatibility and cleaning-state field.

## GC2kp--GC2kt

1. The two simple directed unit paths have a unique maximal common contiguous directed segment containing the collision arc.
2. Entry, exit, complete edge word, both cause terminals and side labels form its canonical geometric address.
3. The segment either begins at a physical source and later splits, or begins internally with distinct incoming edges and ends with distinct outgoing edges, giving a merge--shared-segment--split witness. Distinct cause terminals force the split before the terminal edge.
4. Grouping by complete segment signature preserves multiplicity. If `M` collision witnesses realize `Q` signatures, one signature occurs at least `ceil(M/Q)` times.
5. A donor, remedy, height or incidence-separation estimate excluding the retained segment excludes the witness. Otherwise its shared capacity must be paid by the named physical or height lineage. Omitted paths or remedies, merged sources, changed cleaning state or independent capacity reuse is a reset.

## Consequence

The remaining GC obstruction is one singleton cause shortage or one completely addressed pair of cause paths sharing a canonical physical, remedy or height segment.

## Scope

This is conditional on the complete fixed cleaning network and canonical unit-path decomposition. It does not prove GC5 or the no-three-in-line conjecture.
