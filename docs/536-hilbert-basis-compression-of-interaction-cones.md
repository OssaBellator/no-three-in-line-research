# Hilbert-basis compression of interaction cones

`docs/530` represents tied-critical interaction frontiers by repeated Minkowski
sums. Those sums can still grow with the side length. When the lifted critical
vectors form a normal affine semigroup, a finite Hilbert basis replaces repeated
frontier generation by a lattice-point description and an Ehrhart count.

A critical block `i` has length `ell_i`, common mean work `mu`, and nonnegative
secondary vector `v_i`. Lift it to

```text
g_i=(ell_i,v_i).
```

Let `Lambda` be the integer lattice generated by the `g_i`, `C` their rational
cone, and `S` their nonnegative integer semigroup.

## 1. Normality certificate

### Theorem PP3cmi -- PROVED / CRITICAL HILBERT-BASIS NORMALITY

The cone-lattice intersection `C cap Lambda` has a finite Hilbert basis `H`. If
every element of `H` belongs to `S`—in particular, if the stored critical blocks
contain `H`—then

```text
S=C cap Lambda.
```

Thus every lattice point allowed by the critical cone has an exact critical-block
realization.

#### Proof

By definition, the Hilbert basis is the unique minimal finite generating set of
the additive monoid `C cap Lambda`. If its elements lie in `S`, then every
cone-lattice point is a nonnegative sum of elements of `S`, hence lies in `S`.
The reverse inclusion is immediate. ∎

## 2. Exact frontier slices

### Theorem PP3cmj -- PROVED / NORMAL INTERACTION SLICE

Assume secondary vectors satisfy a positive conservation law
`h(v_i)=ell_i`. Distinct vectors in one fixed-length slice are then
coordinatewise incomparable whenever `h` has strictly positive coefficients.
Under `PP3cmi`, the complete critical minimum-work frontier at length `N` is
exactly

```text
{v : (N,v) in C cap Lambda}.
```

With noncritical blocks of positive reduced work, the eventual full frontier is
a finite union of translations of such normal slices by bounded residue
correctors.

#### Proof

Normality converts every lattice slice point into a critical schedule. All
critical schedules have work `mu N`. The positive conservation law prevents one
distinct slice vector from dominating another. Positive noncritical reduced
work bounds corrector content exactly as in `PP3clq`, leaving finitely many
translations. ∎

## 3. Ehrhart and mesh certificates

### Theorem PP3cmk -- PROVED / INTERACTION EHRHART FRONTIER

For fixed residue data, the number of vectors in the normal slice is an Ehrhart
quasipolynomial in `N`; its generating function is rational. After normalization
by `N`, the lattice frontiers approach the rational critical polytope with
`O(1/N)` mesh. Exact Hilbert-basis and fundamental-parallelepiped checks form a
finite verifier.

#### Proof

A fixed-length cone slice is a rational polytope dilation intersected with a
fixed lattice, so Ehrhart theory gives a quasipolynomial count and rational
generating function. Rounding barycentric coordinates changes the lifted vector
by a bounded lattice amount, yielding `O(1/N)` normalized error. ∎

## 4. Stored exact fixture

The audit `scripts/check_normal_interaction_semigroups.py` uses

```text
P: length 1, work 1, vector (1,0),
Q: length 2, work 2, vector (0,2),
R: length 1, work 2, vector (0,1).
```

`P` and `Q` are critical. Their lifted vectors

```text
(1,1,0), (2,0,2)
```

form the Hilbert basis in their generated lattice. At every length `N`, minimum
work is `N` and the complete frontier is

```text
{(N-2b,2b): 0<=b<=floor(N/2)}.
```

Its size is `floor(N/2)+1`, its length generating function is
`1/((1-z)(1-z^2))`, and its normalized segment mesh is at most `2/N`. The audit
checks every length through 300.

## 5. Prime-patching consequence

Multi-critical interaction frontiers need not be generated one Minkowski power
at a time. A normality certificate turns the whole family into exact lattice
slices with rational generating functions and explicit finite-size mesh bounds.
