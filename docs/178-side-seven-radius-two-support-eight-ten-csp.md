## 3. Revised exact boundary

### Corollary PX527 -- PROVED REDUCTION

If a side-seven full-selector template exists, then either:

1. its selector has distance two from the certified centre and
   
   \[
   |F\triangle F_0|\ge12;
   \]

### Corollary PX528 -- PROVED REDUCTION

The immediate exact coordinate target is the 7,396-selector support-twelve
stratum.  This is the first layer not covered by the radius-one and
support-eight/ten certificates.

The failure of the smaller classes is not monotone in class size: the
`(3,2,2)` support-ten stratum has the largest CSP total despite having fewer
selectors than the combined long-cycle strata.  Future caching should therefore
share geometric line masks and partial coordinate domains, not merely divide
work by selector count.
