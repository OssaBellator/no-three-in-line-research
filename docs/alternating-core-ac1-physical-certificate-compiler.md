# Alternating-core AC1 physical certificate compiler

## Status

This note keeps AC as the sole active research focus and proves AC5lf--AC5lk. It continues the physical AN-bank seed manifest by attaching complete physical addresses and semantic continuation certificates to the exact rank-one through rank-three collateral triples in a nonimproving AN bank.

The result does not prove the missing arithmetic conversion from every heavy labelled family to a quotient, carry or bounded-denominator structure. It proves that the complete occurrence multiset, normalized rank selection, anchor-link extraction and every accepted continuation are finite and auditable. A heavy family without its required structural certificate is returned as the exact next AC1 construction target.

## Complete certificate occurrences

Fix one accepted AN seed and its post-switch fixed set `Z`. A rank-`r` collateral certificate occurrence, `1<=r<=3`, retains:

1. one globally unique occurrence address;
2. exactly `r` mutually compatible nonforbidden anchor-block cells;
3. exactly `3-r` physical cells of `Z`;
4. the real collinear triple and its full line occurrence;
5. endpoint layer and ordered channel word;
6. quotient-ratio cell and source-coset data when present;
7. product-, cross-, coordinate-carry and wrap-centre labels when present;
8. determinant numerator/denominator occurrences when present;
9. current owner, destruction weight and transition-relative status row;
10. seed, bank, matching and epoch provenance.

Distinct three-point subsets of one rich line remain distinct certificate occurrences. No line is collapsed to one Boolean event.

For rank `r`, let `F_r` be the occurrence multiset, counted with this exact multiplicity, and put

\[
T_r=|\mathcal F_r|,
\qquad
U_r=\frac{T_r}{(t)_r}.
\]

## AC5lg -- canonical normalized heavy rank and heavy label -- PROVED

Assume

\[
G=D_\star-F_\star>0
\]

and every AN installation state is nonimproving. Then

\[
128(U_1+U_2+U_3)\ge G.
\]

Let `r_*` be the least rank attaining the maximum normalized mass. Then

\[
\boxed{U_{r_*}\ge G/384.}
\]

Partition `F_(r_*)` by the complete first-stage physical label. If `L` labels occur, let `lambda_*` be the least label of maximum occurrence count. Its normalized weight satisfies

\[
\boxed{
\frac{|\mathcal F_{r_*,\lambda_*}|}{(t)_{r_*}}
\ge
\frac{G}{384L}.
}
\]

The output retains every occurrence in the selected family, not only its cardinality or normalized weight.

### Proof

This is AC1a with deterministic tie-breaking. One of the three normalized masses is at least one third of their sum, and one of `L` label classes carries at least one `L`-th of the selected rank. QED.

## AC5lh -- exact incidence identities and anchor-link router -- PROVED

For one selected rank-`r` occurrence family, define `d(v)` and `d(u,v)` using exact certificate occurrences. Then

\[
\sum_vd(v)=r|\mathcal F|,
\qquad
\sum_{\{u,v\}}d(u,v)=\binom r2|\mathcal F|.
\]

For rank three, fix the least maximum-degree anchor `v`. For every integer `Delta>=1`, exactly one canonical route is returned:

1. the least second anchor `x` with `d(v,x)>Delta`;
2. a greedily selected endpoint-disjoint star through `v` of size at least
   \[
   \boxed{d(v)/(2\Delta-1)}.
   \]

With certificate weights, a heaviest link-edge colour class gives an endpoint-disjoint star retaining at least `1/(2Delta-1)` of the total weight through `v`.

Rich-line occurrences contribute separately to all displayed sums.

### Proof

Double-count incidence pairs and anchor-pair incidences. The link graph at `v` has second-anchor degrees `d(v,x)`. If no degree exceeds `Delta`, greedy matching or `2Delta-1` edge colouring gives the stated unweighted or weighted star. QED.

## Four semantic continuation certificates

A heavy labelled family may continue only through one of the following complete records.

### Anchor continuation

Retain an exact endpoint-disjoint certificate star, its common physical anchor, current destroyed-incidence weights and the complete AN seed fields required to build a new neutralization bank.

### Quotient continuation

Retain the exact physical ratio set, a proved small-quotient or quotient-cell bound, the current paid occurrence subfamily and its total destroyed weight. A large unweighted ratio set is insufficient.

### Carry continuation

Retain the complete carry or wrap-centre signature, its physical producing occurrences, one exact `new` or `repeated` status relative to the monotone exposure ledger and the transition-relative owner route. A repeated signature is routed to recurrence, not counted as new progress.

### BDA continuation

Retain one determinant-realized reduced denominator, the determinant occurrences, exact perfect chamber or wall/radial alternative, physical owner rows and every current paid occurrence assigned to the chamber.

## AC5li -- sound typed continuation normal form -- PROVED

The compiler accepts a heavy family as:

1. `install-seed` only with the complete anchor continuation;
2. `paid-ratio-bank` only with the complete quotient continuation;
3. `new-signature` or `repeated-signature` only with the complete carry continuation;
4. `bda-chamber` only with the complete BDA continuation.

Missing one required field returns the heavy occurrence family, the intended route and the least missing semantic certificate. Two routes may not claim the same occurrence weight unless their capacity record explicitly splits or shares it under one physical ledger.

### Proof

Each route is defined by a finite required-field dictionary. The historical AN, WQ/CR, carry-exposure and BDA interfaces give meaning to the four accepted records. Their omitted hypotheses are not consequences of label equality, so fail-closed rejection is necessary. QED.

### Proof

### Proof
