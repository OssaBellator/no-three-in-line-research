# Geometric-cleaning cut-arc collision pairs

This note refines the canonical physical/remedy/height cut-pressure theorem. It turns every named positive-pressure arc into explicit pairs of separately payable cause units that both require that exact cleaning capacity.

## Contract

Let `C=A disjoint_union B` be the canonical split of a non-singleton irreducible cause core. Choose deterministic integral full flows for the two proper subsets, cancel directed cycles lexicographically, and decompose the remaining flows into ordered unit physical-source-to-cause paths. Every path retains its cause, remedy, height, donor and physical-source addresses.

For a forward arc `e` of the canonical cleaning minimum cut with capacity `c_e`, put

`p_e=f_A(e)+f_B(e)-c_e>0`.

## GC2kk--GC2ko

1. The cycle-cancelled side flows have canonical unit-path decompositions, with exactly `f_A(e)` and `f_B(e)` cause paths through `e`.
2. Since each side separately respects capacity, `p_e<=min(f_A(e),f_B(e))`. Oppositely ordered allocation to the `c_e` unit slots gives exactly `p_e` doubly occupied slots.
3. Every doubly occupied slot is an ordered pair of exact cause paths sharing the named physical, remedy or height arc. The least slot gives a canonical geometric collision witness with both complete path addresses.
4. If the colliding paths use `a_e` cause classes on side `A` and `b_e` classes on side `B`, one ordered class pair occurs at least `ceil(p_e/(a_e b_e))` times.
5. A geometric, clean-height or donor-separation argument excluding all retained cross-side pairs, or bounding all class-pair collision multiplicities below the required total, excludes the core. Singleton cause cores remain direct shortages. Omitted remedies or paths, merged sources, uncancelled circulation, changed cleaning state or independent capacity reuse is a reset.

## Consequence

The remaining GC obstruction is one direct cause shortage or one exact pair of separately payable cleaning paths colliding on a named physical/remedy/height capacity.

## Scope

This is conditional on the complete fixed cleaning network and deterministic integral flows. It does not construct the geometric capacities and does not prove GC5 or the no-three-in-line conjecture.
