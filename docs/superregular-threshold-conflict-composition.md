# SRR threshold transportation and conflict composition

This note composes the endpoint-cost threshold router with the exact conflict-atom degree bound. It does not prove SRR2, SRR4, or the no-three-in-line conjecture.

## Threshold candidate family

Every feasible switching candidate `v` has:

- nonnegative weight `w_v`;
- endpoint cost `c_v`; and
- a complete finite conflict-atom support `S_v`.

For a threshold `t`, let `V_t={v:c_v<=t}`. The earlier transportation theorems provide the total retained candidate weight in `V_t` after conditioning and reverse-load losses.

## Exact pairwise conflict graph

Assume two candidates conflict exactly when their atom supports intersect. For each atom `a`, let

`lambda_a(t)=|{v in V_t:a in S_v}|`.

Then every candidate degree satisfies

`deg_t(v) <= sum_{a in S_v}(lambda_a(t)-1)`.

## Executable low-cost family

The weighted local-minimum lemma gives an executable independent subfamily of `V_t` with weight at least

`sum_{v in V_t} w_v/(deg_t(v)+1)`.

Combining the atom bound yields the fully local estimate

`sum_{v in V_t} w_v / (1+sum_{a in S_v}(lambda_a(t)-1))`.

If every support has size at most `r` and every threshold atom load is at most `Lambda`, the executable low-cost weight is at least

`W_t/[1+r(Lambda-1)]`,

where `W_t=sum_{v in V_t}w_v`.

## Composition with endpoint cost

The estimate holds at every endpoint-cost threshold. Thus the threshold Hall-deficiency layer cake first supplies low-cost candidate mass, and conflict thinning then retains a deterministic executable fraction without changing the endpoint threshold.

A failed final bound now identifies one of two exact witnesses:

- a threshold forward/reverse/conditioning deficiency; or
- a heavy exact conflict atom.

## Outside the contract

The composition does not cover conflicts omitted from the atom dictionary, higher-order incompatibilities not generated by pairwise intersections, dynamic supports, or execution rules that create new conflicts after the independent family is selected.
