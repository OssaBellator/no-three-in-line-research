# Canonical reconstruction of prime-field RI quotient labels

**Branch:** `research/alternating-core-chain`

AC3mv leaves abstract rational-inverse subgroup, coset, order and root labels outside
the physical determinant-rational BDA bound.  For the prime-field RI routes used by
AC3at--AC3be, most of those labels are not independent data.  The multiplicative
group is cyclic, a subgroup is determined by its order, every coset has a canonical
exponent address, and the root, image and scale cosets are reconstructed from the
physical factor.

This note gives the exact reconstruction and polynomial stocks.  It does not turn a
repeated quotient address into payment or progress.  Component selection, physical
occurrence ancestry, owner assignment and completion payability remain separate.

## Prime-field address convention

Fix an odd prime `p`, put

\[
N=p-1,
\]

and fix once and for all the least primitive root `gamma` of `F_p^*`.  For every
divisor `h | N`, put

\[
k=N/h,
\qquad
H_h=\langle \gamma^k\rangle.
\]

The quotient `F_p^*/H_h` has order `k`.

## AC3mw -- subgroup order is a complete address -- PROVED

For every divisor `h | N`, `H_h` is the unique multiplicative subgroup of
`F_p^*` of order `h`.  Every multiplicative subgroup is one of these groups.
Consequently an RI subgroup label is represented canonically by the integer `h`,
and the total subgroup stock is

\[
\boxed{\tau(N)\le N.}
\]

### Proof

The multiplicative group of a finite prime field is cyclic of order `N`.  In a
cyclic group there is exactly one subgroup of every order dividing the group
order, generated here by `gamma^(N/h)`.  The divisor count is at most `N`. QED.

The theorem uses the fixed prime field.  A subgroup of an unrelated ambient group,
or a subgroup label without the prime-field realization, is not covered.

## AC3mx -- canonical coset exponent addresses -- PROVED

For `h | N` and `k=N/h`, every coset of `H_h` has one unique address

\[
\boxed{(h,j),\qquad 0\le j<k,}
\]

meaning the coset `gamma^j H_h`.  In these addresses,

\[
(\gamma^iH_h)(\gamma^jH_h)=\gamma^{i+j}H_h,
\qquad
(\gamma^iH_h)^{-1}=\gamma^{-i}H_h,
\]

with exponents read modulo `k`.

The complete subgroup-coset address stock is

\[
\boxed{
C_{\rm cos}(N)
=
\sum_{h\mid N}\frac Nh
=
\sigma(N)
\le
N\tau(N)
\le
N^2.
}
\]

### Proof

The quotient is cyclic, generated by `gamma H_h`, and has order `k`.  Its cosets
are therefore exactly `gamma^jH_h`, `0<=j<k`, with the displayed arithmetic.
Summing `k=N/h` over divisors gives `sigma(N)`.  Each summand is at most `N`, and
there are `tau(N)` summands. QED.

No discrete logarithm complexity claim is needed: this is a mathematical address
dictionary.  Implementations may use the actual coset set or its least field
element instead.

## Physical RI occurrences

Retain the AC3ay notation.  Let nonzero field elements `a,b,x,u,z` satisfy

\[
r=b/a\ne1,
\qquad
c=z/x\notin\{1,r\},
\qquad
 g=u/x=F_r(c)=\frac{c(1-c)}{r-c}.
\]

Define

\[
c^\dagger=\tau_r(c)=\frac{r(c-1)}{c-r}.
\]

For one subgroup order `h`, let `R,A,B,C,S` denote the canonical exponent
addresses of `r,c,c^dagger,g,x`, respectively.

## AC3my -- physical factors reconstruct every root, image and scale coset -- PROVED

The physical tuple `(a,b,x,u,z)` determines

\[
\boxed{r,c,g,c^\dagger}
\]

and, for every declared subgroup order `h`, determines the quotient addresses
`R,A,B,C,S`.  They satisfy

\[
\boxed{B=R+C-A\pmod{k},}
\]

and the physical partner and anchor cosets have addresses

\[
\boxed{uH_h=C+S,\qquad zH_h=A+S\pmod{k}.}
\]

Thus the root cosets, rational image coset, companion root coset and base-scale
coset are derived data.  They must not be retained as additional independent
outer-profile coordinates once the physical occurrence and `h` are fixed.

### Proof

Field division reconstructs `r=b/a`, `c=z/x` and `g=u/x`.  The rational formula
reconstructs `c^dagger`.  AC3ay gives

\[
cc^\dagger=rg,
\]

which becomes `A+B=R+C` in exponent addresses and hence the first display.
The identities `u=gx` and `z=cx` give the physical coset displays. QED.

The exclusions `r!=1` and `c notin {1,r}` remove the degenerate zero or undefined
rational-map cases.  Any exceptional route must retain its existing explicit
normalization-conflict label.

## Normalized component dictionaries

A normalized RI component uses at most four source cosets.  Retain one finite
external role word containing channel order, component type, carry word, blocker
occupancy and the already proved finite rank/closure decorations.  Let its stock
be `L_RI`.

For a subgroup of index `k`, encode a scale-free normalized component by

\[
(h,m,U_1,\ldots,U_m,R,A,C,\lambda),
\qquad 1\le m\le4,
\]

where `B` is reconstructed from `R,C,A`.  A scale-localized component additionally
retains `S`.

## AC3mz -- polynomial normalized RI component stock -- PROVED

The complete scale-free normalized component stock is at most

\[
\boxed{
N_{\rm RI}^{\rm norm}
\le
L_{\rm RI}
\sum_{h\mid N}\sum_{m=1}^4 (N/h)^{m+3}
\le
4L_{\rm RI}N^8.
}
\]

The complete scale-localized stock is at most

\[
\boxed{
N_{\rm RI}^{\rm scale}
\le
L_{\rm RI}
\sum_{h\mid N}\sum_{m=1}^4 (N/h)^{m+4}
\le
4L_{\rm RI}N^9.
}
\]

The bounds are safe ambient stocks: the source cosets are actually distinct and
many tuples do not satisfy the RI compatibility relation.

### Proof

For fixed `h`, each quotient coordinate has `k=N/h` choices.  A scale-free word
uses at most seven quotient coordinates: four source cosets and `R,A,C`.  Adding
`S` gives at most eight.  Sum the four possible source counts, bound every power
by `N^7` or `N^8`, and use `tau(N)<=N`. QED.

## AC3na -- polynomial physical RI occurrence addresses -- PROVED

Suppose one exact occurrence role is determined by the finite word `lambda` and
the physical tuple `(a,b,x,u,z)`, with every element nonzero.  Including the
subgroup order, the ambient physical occurrence stock is at most

\[
\boxed{
N_{\rm RI}^{\rm occ}
\le
L_{\rm RI}\tau(N)N^5
\le
L_{\rm RI}N^6.
}
\]

Every valid tuple reconstructs all quotient fields through AC3my.  A weighted
family of total occurrence weight `W` therefore contains one exact physical
occurrence address of weight at least

\[
\boxed{W/N_{\rm RI}^{\rm occ}.}
\]

### Proof

Choose `h`, the five nonzero physical field elements and the finite role word.
The validity equations only reduce this ambient product.  Weighted pigeonhole
gives the final statement. QED.

Historical weight on one occurrence address is not simultaneous payment.  The
current owner and faithful destruction requirements of AC3jv--AC3jz remain in
force.

## AC3nb -- corrected RI quotient outer-profile interface -- PROVED UNDER THE PRIME-FIELD PHYSICAL CONTRACT

For canonical prime-field RI routes with a fixed primitive-root convention:

1. subgroup identity is reconstructed from its order by AC3mw;
2. every quotient coset has the canonical address of AC3mx;
3. root, image, companion and scale cosets are reconstructed from the physical
   occurrence and subgroup order by AC3my;
4. normalized components and scale-localized components have the polynomial
   stocks of AC3mz;
5. exact physical occurrence addresses have the polynomial stock of AC3na;
6. none of those derived labels contributes an independent set or symbolic-name
   multiplier to the AC3ka outer profile.

The following data remain separate:

- the choice or generator of the source-coset family;
- the exact set of physical occurrences assigned to one component;
- scale-coherence and component-payability failures;
- component, owner and capacity assignments;
- an external role dictionary without a polynomial `L_RI` bound;
- non-prime-field or unrelated abstract quotient groups;
- recurrence of one exact component or occurrence address.

A repeated exact RI address enters AC3kz--AC3lc.  Equality of quotient labels is
not current payment, arithmetic descent or a capacity-one ticket.

### Proof

Items 1--5 are AC3mw--AC3na.  Each reconstructed field is a deterministic function
of data already retained, so storing it again duplicates the outer profile.  The
remaining list contains choices or hypotheses not determined by those data and
therefore cannot be deleted.  The recurrence warning is AC3kd and AC3lc. QED.

## Consequence

The abstract subgroup/coset naming portion of the canonical prime-field RI route
is no longer an unbounded AC4 alphabet.  In the prime-minus-one regime `N=p-1=n`,
its normalized scale-localized stock is `O(L_RI n^9)` and its exact physical
occurrence stock is `O(L_RI n^6)`.

The remaining RI recurrence is now attached to exact component generators,
physical occurrence assignments, owner/payability routes and repeated polynomially
indexed addresses rather than unnamed subgroup or root symbols.
