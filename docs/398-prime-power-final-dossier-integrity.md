# Final dossier integrity beyond the implication-premise contract

CMR2342--CMR2349 assemble ten ordinary implication premises above the semantic closure dossier.
CMR2350--CMR2357 separately require noncircular dependency support among the typed obligation
artifacts. This chapter composes those endpoints with typed artifacts for the ten final premises
into one pre-root documentary integrity gate.

## Theorem CMR2358 — PROVED AS AN INTERFACE

The final integrity certificate contains exactly:

1. one CMR2342--CMR2349 final implication-premise contract;
2. one typed final-premise artifact registry; and
3. one CMR2350--CMR2357 obligation-artifact support-DAG certificate.

The premise registry must bind the exact contract, and all three certificates must use the
identical typed obligation-artifact registry.

## Theorem CMR2359 — PROVED

Every declared proved final premise has exactly one premise-specific artifact kind. Its artifact
must cite obligation artifacts covering every semantic dependency of that premise, and the
reconstructed premise-bundle digest must equal the locator/digest pair stored in the contract.

For `edgewise-lex` termination, the premise artifact additionally binds the exact edgewise
support certificate. Open premises may not contain placeholder artifacts.

## Theorem CMR2360 — PROVED

The obligation-artifact support integrity gate is one exactly when the support graph is acyclic,
dependency-aligned and complete over every immediate prerequisite artifact bundle of each
proved obligation.

## Theorem CMR2361 — PROVED

The final contract gate is inherited exactly from CMR2347. It requires the finite interface
gate, typed obligation-artifact coverage, closure of every dependency of
`GLOBAL_QUOTIENT_IMPLIES_ALL_N`, an admissible termination mode and all ten implication
premises effectively closed.

The root implication obligation itself may remain open and actionable. Consequently the
combined integrity dossier is a pre-root handoff rather than a certificate that presupposes
the desired implication.

## Theorem CMR2362 — PROVED

The combined integrity gate is

\[
\boxed{
\texttt{final\_dossier\_integrity\_ready}
=
\texttt{final\_implication\_contract\_ready}
\land
\texttt{typed\_premise\_artifact\_coverage\_ready}
\land
\texttt{obligation\_artifact\_support\_integrity\_ready}.
}
\]

A final dossier cannot become ready while a premise artifact is missing or unbound, or while its
obligation-artifact support is circular, downstream or incomplete.

## Theorem CMR2363 — PROVED

The checker publishes the exact open premise IDs, premise-artifact count, any proved obligations
with incomplete artifact support, the maximum artifact-support depth and the digests of the
contract, premise registry, support DAG, shared obligation registry and final gate record.

It permanently publishes

\[
\boxed{\texttt{all\_n\_proved\_by\_checker}=0.}
\]

No documentary readiness flag is reinterpreted as a machine proof of the conjecture.

## Theorem CMR2364 — HONEST FINAL INTEGRITY BOUNDARY

A ready integrity dossier would establish exact internal linkage, typed final-premise coverage,
noncircular documentary support and closure of every declared proof premise needed before the
root implication. It would not close that root or verify the mathematical truth, completeness or
sufficiency of the human-readable proof artifacts.

## Corollary CMR2365 — EXECUTABLE ENDPOINT

`scripts/check_prime_power_premise_artifact_registry.py` validates typed artifacts for all ten
final premises. `scripts/check_prime_power_final_dossier_integrity.py` composes that registry
with the final implication contract and canonical obligation-artifact support DAG over one
shared typed obligation registry.

The two artifact-support command names now delegate to one canonical exact-coverage algorithm.
The checkers were syntax-compiled in the publication environment. With the current open semantic
obligations, the final integrity gate remains zero.
