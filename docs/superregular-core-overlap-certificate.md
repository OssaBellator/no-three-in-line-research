# Superregular irreducible burden-core overlap

This note continues the canonical irreducible burden-core result on the same complete finite physical/atom/burden network. It identifies the exact shared-capacity competition that the remaining geometric argument must exclude or pay.

## Contract

For a burden set `S`, let `d(S)` be total witnessed burden, `F(S)` the maximum physical-source/atom flow with only burdens in `S` enabled, and `Delta(S)=d(S)-F(S)`. Retain every conditioned threshold, candidate, witness atom, physical source, capacity and compatibility address.

Let `C` be the canonical minimum-cardinality deficient burden core and put `delta=Delta(C)>0`. Every proper subset of `C` is fully payable.

## SRR2dj--SRR2dn

1. Every nonempty proper `A subset C` satisfies `F(A)=d(A)`.
2. Every nontrivial partition `C=A disjoint_union B` has exact competition `Omega(A,B)=F(A)+F(B)-F(C)=delta`.
3. If `|C|>=2`, the least balanced complete witnessed-address split is a canonical atom-capacity competition witness of size `delta`; a singleton is already a direct local burden shortage.
4. Any geometric tensor or incidence decomposition producing additive payment across one retained split, or an overlap upper bound below `delta`, rules out the core.
5. Omitted conflicts or witnesses, merged atom or physical-source capacity, changed conditioning or threshold, or independent reuse of shared capacity is a reset.

## Consequence

The remaining SRR obstruction is either one direct witnessed burden shortage or two separately payable burden families whose physical-source/atom optima overlap by exactly the whole deficit. The next geometric step is to separate a canonical split, bound its overlap below `delta`, or charge the exact overlap to a named physical source or witness atom.

## Scope

This theorem is conditional on the complete fixed network and exact subset max-flow values. It does not prove SRR2, SRR4 or the no-three-in-line conjecture.
