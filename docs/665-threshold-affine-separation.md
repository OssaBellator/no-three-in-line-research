# Threshold affine separation

The previous threshold chapters show that the eight nearest legal targets have
sign-coherent drift and that perfect buffer and exposure balancing still exposes
illegal intermediates. This chapter proves a global endpoint obstruction for the
entire legal four-layer catalogue.

For a `4 x 4` matrix `M`, define

```text
Phi(M) = M[0,3] + M[1,0] - M[1,1] - M[2,2] + M[3,0] + M[3,2].
```

## PP3dbf — Exact affine separator

The threshold source matrix

```text
2 1 1 0
0 2 1 1
1 0 2 1
1 1 0 2
```

has score

```text
Phi(SOURCE) = -3.
```

There are eighteen no-three permutation layers and 4,475 distinct matrices made
from multisets of four such layers. Every legal four-layer matrix has nonnegative
score. The exact score distribution is

```text
0:495, 1:956, 2:1193, 3:1012, 4:590,
5:176, 6:44, 7:8, 8:1.
```

At layer level the score distribution is `0:9, 1:8, 2:1`, so nonnegativity is
also visible before layers are aggregated.

## PP3dbg — No finite legal endpoint compensation

No nonempty multiset of legal four-layer matrices has average equal to the source
matrix. Equivalently, for no positive integer `k` do legal matrices
`M_1,...,M_k` satisfy

```text
M_1 + ... + M_k = k*SOURCE.
```

### Proof

Applying `Phi` to the right-hand side gives `-3k`. Applying `Phi` to the left-hand
side gives a nonnegative integer because every legal matrix has nonnegative
score. These values cannot agree. ∎

Thus the source lies outside both the convex hull and the nonnegative rational
cone slice generated by legal four-layer endpoints. The obstruction is not
bounded by walk length.

## PP3dbh — Consequence for native compensation schemes

The eight nearest legal targets all have score zero, so each native target move
raises `Phi` by exactly three from the source. Distinct buffers, balanced exposure,
three-round factorizations, and arbitrary finite mixtures of legal endpoint
matrices cannot restore the missing score.

Any successful threshold mechanism must therefore do at least one of the
following:

1. change the source matrix;
2. enlarge the allowed endpoint layer catalogue;
3. use a hidden state whose effective endpoint is not a legal four-layer matrix;
4. couple threshold transport to a different geometric operation carrying
   negative `Phi`.

`scripts/check_threshold_affine_separation.py` enumerates all eighteen legal
permutation layers and all 4,475 legal four-layer matrices, verifies the exact
score distributions, and checks the eight nearest targets.

## Evidence boundary

This is an exact matrix-level impossibility theorem. It does not rule out a
coordinate construction that changes the source, enlarges the layer repertoire,
or couples threshold transport to an external compensating mechanism.
