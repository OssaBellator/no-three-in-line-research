# Bounded-depth final-shadow cascades

The two-step path-independence theorem PP3ahv--PP3aia shows that direct final
allocation ignores every blocker incidence supported only in an intermediate
source.  The same statement holds for an arbitrary finite sequence of
source-valid controller-preserving trades.

This matters for the unary source-star conversion.  A trade that destroys a
controller-domain margin may create a new source-star centre; moving that centre
may create another unary concentration.  It is unnecessary to pay the full
intermediate `Xi` cost at every generation.  For direct allocation, retain only
the final source and test its final simple support.

If the final source contains `S` genuinely new points relative to the initial
source, all final binary shadow removes at most `S(S-1)` values from one paired
macro domain.  Thus a fixed or slowly growing cascade remains harmless whenever
`S^2=o(R)`.  Final unary support either fits the remaining margin or creates the
next source-star centre with degree `Omega(R/S)`.

## 1. A source-valid trade path

Let

```text
S_j=(S_{j-1}\D_j) union P_j,
1<=j<=t,
```

where every state `S_j` is no-three-in-line and every trade preserves the
controller, saturation, and matching-layer infrastructure required by the next
trade.

Define the final retained and final new sets

```text
O_t=S_t cap S_0,
N_t=S_t\S_0,
S=|N_t|.
```

A point inserted at some stage and absent from `S_t` is **transient**.

### Proposition PP3aib -- PROVED

Every final blocker pair not contained in `O_t` has exactly one of the forms

```text
N_t--O_t
```

or

```text
N_t--N_t.
```

No transient point contributes to the final candidate-entry support.
Consequently the final support is determined solely by `(O_t,N_t)`, independent
of the length and internal geometry of the trade path.

#### Proof

Partition `S_t` by membership in `S_0`, exactly as in PP3ahv.  A transient point
is absent from `S_t`.  Candidate-domain membership is tested against blocker
pairs in the final source, so no path data remain. ∎

This is a path-independence statement for final domains, not for the integer
potential evaluated at intermediate states.

## 2. Cumulative final domain loss

Let `U_t` be the final unary `Xi` incidence weight generated by pairs with one
endpoint in `N_t` and one in `O_t`.  Let

```text
d_M^t(i,A), d_F^t(i,B)
```

be the corresponding simple unary degrees in one macro and at one final label.

### Theorem PP3aic -- PROVED

For every macro and final label pair, the complete final unary and binary shadow
removes at most

```text
d_M^t(i,A)+d_F^t(i,B)+S(S-1)
```

values from the paired macro domain.  In particular the label-free bound is

```text
U_t+S(S-1).
```

The bound contains no sum of intermediate costs.

#### Proof

Apply PP3ahj to the final unary support `N_t--O_t`.  Apply PP3ahc to the final
inserted set `N_t`, which gives binary loss at most `S(S-1)`.  Proposition
PP3aib removes all transient supports. ∎

The same final candidate entry may have had arbitrarily large multiplicity in
several intermediate states; it is deleted at most once from the final domain.

## 3. Active-scale depth budget

At the slab scale,

```text
R=m^(19/20+o(1)).
```

Suppose trade `j` inserts at most `s_j` points.  Then

```text
S <= sum_{j=1}^t s_j.
```

### Corollary PP3aid -- PROVED

If

```text
sum_j s_j = m^(kappa_*+o(1))
```

with

```text
kappa_*<19/40,
```

then

```text
S^2/R=o(1).
```

In particular, if every trade inserts at most

```text
s=m^(kappa+o(1))
```

and the depth satisfies

```text
t=m^(delta+o(1)),
kappa+delta<19/40,
```

then every fixed positive base-domain margin absorbs all final binary shadow of
the complete cascade.

#### Proof

Use `S<=sum_j s_j`.  The exponent of `S^2/R` is at most

```text
2kappa_*-19/20<0.
```

For the second statement, `sum_j s_j<=ts` has exponent at most
`kappa+delta`. ∎

Thus bounded depth is far from the limit: a polynomially growing number of very
small marked trades is allowed as long as the total surviving size remains
below the square-root domain scale.

## 4. Direct cascade completion or another star

Fix `gamma,xi>0`.  Let `H_i^base(A,B)` be the final domain after testing `O_t`
and all source, anchor, transition, and nonshadow exclusions.  Assume the margin
graphs `J_i^base(gamma+xi)` satisfy PP3fy or PP3gl and the local width condition
PP3ho holds.

### Theorem PP3aie -- PROVED / CONDITIONAL CASCADE INTERFACE

At least one of the following occurs.

1. For every macro and label pair,

   ```text
   d_M^t(i,A)+d_F^t(i,B)+S(S-1) <= xi R,
   ```

   and the final patch completes directly through the same balanced ownership
   and global matching.
2. Final binary support alone exceeds the available margin, which requires
   `S^2=Omega(R)`.
3. Final unary support destroys a margin-compatible domain, and one point of
   `N_t` is the centre of a final source-star fibre of size

   ```text
   C > xi R/(2S).
   ```
4. The base domain margin, global allocation criterion, local width condition,
   or controller-preserving source-valid trade path fails.

#### Proof

Alternative 1 is PP3ahk applied with Theorem PP3aic.  If the binary term exceeds
`xi R/2`, then `S(S-1)=Omega(R)`.  Otherwise any failed margin is caused by more
than a constant fraction of `xi R` unary deletions.  Split those deletions among
the at most `2S` final point/type classes and apply PP3aho. ∎

At the active scales of PP3aid, alternative 2 is absent.  The only final-shadow
failure is another explicit source star.

## 5. No intermediate credit is required

The monotone composite theorem PP3ahs assumes growing first-step removal credit
to pay uncancelled collateral.  The direct cascade route has a different
interface.

### Corollary PP3aif -- PROVED

Under alternative 1 of PP3aie, none of the trades in the path needs positive or
growing dynamic removal credit.  Intermediate states may increase `Xi`
arbitrarily.  It is sufficient that:

1. every trade is source-valid and controller-preserving;
2. the final state has robust nonshadow domains;
3. the final support satisfies the displayed margin inequality.

#### Proof

The proof of direct allocation uses only the final domains and never invokes a
potential identity.  Path independence PP3aib discards every intermediate
support. ∎

This removes the “first trade has no growing removal credit” case from the
robust direct-allocation branch.  It remains open in branches where final
allocation cannot yet be invoked.

## 6. Revised cascade endpoint

### Corollary PP3aig -- PROVED

A bounded-depth unary-star or endpoint-trade cascade has the following endpoint.

1. If the total surviving final insertion size has square `o(R)` and final unary
   degrees fit the robust margin, complete directly.
2. If a final unary margin fails, extract a next-generation source star of degree
   `Omega(R/S)`.
3. If the cascade cannot be continued, the obstruction is source/transition,
   anchor, conditional-Hall, alternating-host, distinguished endpoint-host, or
   base allocation failure.
4. Intermediate unary or binary `Xi` multiplicity and intermediate lack of
   removal credit are not independent obstructions.
5. The genuinely large-state boundary is the cumulative condition

   ```text
   S^2=Omega(R),
   ```

   not the size or cost of any one intermediate state.

No termination of an arbitrarily long source-star cascade is claimed.  The new
result removes intermediate collateral bookkeeping and isolates the remaining
problem as final unary concentration, cumulative state growth, or failure of the
underlying source/allocation hosts.

## 7. Finite diagnostic

The script

```text
scripts/check_final_state_shadow_path.py
```

accepts an arbitrary finite list of removals and insertions.  It validates the
no-three condition at every stage, compares the union of all intermediate
candidate supports with the final support, computes the exact final domain loss,
and reports direct completion or the next-generation unary-star alternative.
