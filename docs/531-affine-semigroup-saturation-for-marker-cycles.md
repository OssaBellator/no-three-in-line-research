# Affine-semigroup saturation for mixed-length marker cycles

`docs/525` treats several tied marker cycles through a finite min-plus critical
graph. Its stored critical cycles have a common cyclicity. A boundary controller
may instead contain equally efficient macro blocks of genuinely different
lengths. This chapter replaces a common-period assumption by the numerical
semigroup generated by the critical lengths.

A marker block `i` has positive integer length `ell_i`, rational cost `c_i`, and a
nonnegative rational action vector `a_i`. Put

```text
mu=min_i c_i/ell_i
```

and call `i` critical when `c_i=mu ell_i`. Let `g` be the gcd of the critical
lengths.

## 1. Critical-length saturation

### Theorem PP3clt -- PROVED / CRITICAL NUMERICAL-SEMIGROUP CONDUCTOR

Let `S` be the additive semigroup generated by the critical lengths. There is an
integer conductor `C` such that every multiple of `g` at least `C` belongs to
`S`. A conductor and an explicit critical-block representation for every
residue state can be found by a finite shortest-path computation modulo any one
critical length.

#### Proof

After division by `g`, the critical lengths have gcd one. The semigroup they
generate is therefore a numerical semigroup and has finite complement in the
nonnegative integers. A shortest-path search on residues modulo a chosen
generator records the least represented integer in each residue. Adding copies
of that generator fills every larger integer in the same residue. Scaling back
by `g` gives the claim and constructive representations. ∎

## 2. Mixed-length marker quasipolynomial

### Theorem PP3clu -- PROVED / SEMIGROUP RESIDUE COST FORMULA

Give block `i` reduced cost

```text
r_i=c_i-mu ell_i >= 0.
```

For every attainable residue `r mod g`, there are a finite minimum reduced
excess `eta_r` and threshold `N_r` such that

```text
F(N)=mu N+eta_r
```

for every attainable `N>=N_r` with residue `r`. A minimum schedule is a bounded
residue corrector followed by critical blocks only.

#### Proof

Every noncritical block has strictly positive reduced cost. Hence a
minimum-reduced-cost corrector for a fixed residue uses only a bounded number of
noncritical blocks; otherwise deleting a positive-reduced-cost cycle would
improve it. Once its length is fixed, `PP3clt` supplies all sufficiently large
additional lengths in the zero residue by critical blocks of reduced cost zero.
Thus the minimum excess stabilizes and the displayed affine formula follows. ∎

## 3. Mixed-length critical rate polytope

### Theorem PP3clv -- PROVED / SEMIGROUP RATE-POLYTOPE CERTIFICATE

The limiting action-rate set of minimum-cost schedules is

```text
P=conv{a_i/ell_i : i critical}.
```

For every point of `P` and every sufficiently large attainable length `N`, an
integer critical mixture plus a bounded residue corrector gives a deterministic
schedule whose action-rate error is `O(1/N)`. A finite semigroup table certifies
the conductor, all residue correctors, and every finite mixture used.

#### Proof

Every minimum schedule contains only bounded noncritical content, so its
normalized action vector differs by `O(1/N)` from a convex combination of the
critical rates. Conversely, round the coefficients of any convex combination
to critical-block multiplicities. The resulting length and action errors are
bounded independently of `N`; a stored residue corrector repairs the length.
Division by `N` gives the stated error. ∎

## 4. Stored exact fixture

The audit `scripts/check_mixed_length_marker_semigroups.py` uses

```text
P: length 2, cost 2, action (2,0),
Q: length 3, cost 3, action (0,3),
R: length 1, cost 2, action (1,0).
```

`P` and `Q` are critical, their length semigroup has conductor two, and

```text
F(1)=2,
F(N)=N for every N>=2.
```

At length `N>=2`, the complete minimum-cost rate mesh is obtained from
`2a+3b=N`; its `l_1` covering radius in `conv((1,0),(0,1))` is at most `8/N`.
The audit verifies every length through 500.

## 5. Prime-patching consequence

Boundary controllers no longer need critical blocks of one common period. A
finite conductor table converts mixed block lengths into exact all-large-length
cost formulas and deterministic rate approximations.
