# The installed construction bank has exact owner effects and scheduler payment

This chapter records CMR3008--CMR3021. The branch now contains many literal
construction transitions, but global exhaustiveness must not be inferred merely
from their existence. The present bank performs the intermediate task which is
both meaningful and currently provable: it enumerates every installed transition
kind, binds it to its checker contract and theorem ancestry, records its exact
owner effect, and assigns one finite stock, strict descent, reintroduction charge
or mandatory scheduler continuation.

The executable checker is:

```text
scripts/check_prime_power_installed_owner_scheduler_bank.py
```

## CMR3012 — dispatch operations cannot be terminal

mandatory continuation into an already installed scheduler branch. Empty or
unbounded continuation metadata is rejected.

\[
\mathcal O(d,\lambda)
=
