# Two-step final-state shadow path independence

The composite source-star theorem PP3aho--PP3ahu controls a two-step trade by
adding the two dynamic `Xi` identities.  That route still charges every
uncancelled first-step and second-step insertion incidence.

For the robust final-allocation branch there is a stronger principle: controller
domains depend only on the final source.  A blocker incidence created in the
intermediate state and destroyed by the second trade removes no value from the
final controller-aware domains.  Candidate multiplicity on such a transient
line is irrelevant.

After two source-valid trades, write the final source as the disjoint union of
retained points from the original source and points that survive as genuinely
new final points.  Every blocker pair not already present in the final retained
original source contains at least one genuinely new final point.  Pairs with one
new endpoint are unary final shadow; pairs with two new endpoints are binary
final shadow.  Points inserted and later removed occur in neither class.

Consequently the binary domain loss is controlled only by the number `s_f` of
surviving final inserted points, through the bound `s_f(s_f-1)`.  The unary loss
is the simple final unary support of those same points.  Under a robust base
domain margin, arbitrary intermediate `Xi` cost can therefore be bypassed
without a monotone potential decrease.

## 1. Two source-valid trades

Let `S_0` be the initial saturated no-three source.  Let

```text
S_1=(S_0\D_1) union P_1
```

and

```text
S_2=(S_1\D_2) union P_2
```

be two controller-preserving source-valid trades.  Thus `S_1` and `S_2` are both
no-three-in-line, and every old row/column or matching-layer invariant needed by
the endpoint infrastructure is preserved.

Put

```text
O_2=S_2 cap S_0,
N_2=S_2\S_0,
s_f=|N_2|.
```

The set `O_2` is the final retained original source and `N_2` is the final new
point set.  Points in `P_1\S_2` are **transient first-step points**.

### Proposition PP3ahv -- PROVED

One has the exact disjoint decomposition

```text
S_2=O_2 dot-union N_2.
```

Every unordered blocker pair contained in `S_2` but not contained in `O_2` has
exactly one of the following forms.

1. `new--old`: one endpoint in `N_2` and one endpoint in `O_2`;
2. `new--new`: both endpoints in `N_2`.

No transient first-step point belongs to any final blocker pair.

#### Proof

The first display is the partition of `S_2` according to membership in `S_0`.
A pair not contained in `O_2` has at least one endpoint outside `S_0`, hence in
`N_2`.  It has either one or two such endpoints.  A transient point is absent
from `S_2`, so it cannot occur in a pair contained in `S_2`. ∎

This elementary decomposition is the final-state analogue of the unary/binary
support-rank split used throughout the marked `Xi` analysis.

## 2. Intermediate shadow is erased exactly

Let `Z_j` be the simple controller candidate-entry support created by blocker
pairs in `S_j` that are not already blocker pairs of the retained original
source in that state.  Candidate entries are movement or refill entries in the
controller-edge--label incidence graph.

### Theorem PP3ahw -- PROVED

The final support `Z_2` depends only on `(O_2,N_2)`.  More precisely,

```text
Z_2=Z_un(O_2,N_2) union Z_bin(N_2),
```

where:

- `Z_un(O_2,N_2)` is generated by pairs with one endpoint in each set;
- `Z_bin(N_2)` is generated by pairs of points in `N_2`.

Every entry in

```text
Z_1\Z_2
```

is transient and removes no value from any final controller-aware domain.
This remains true with arbitrary dynamic `Xi` multiplicity on the transient
entry.

#### Proof

A final candidate entry is forbidden exactly when its candidate cell lies on a
blocker line determined by a pair contained in the final source `S_2`.  Apply
PP3ahv to that pair.  Conversely every pair of either displayed type belongs to
`S_2` and contributes its candidate trace.  An entry in `Z_1\Z_2` has no
supporting blocker pair in `S_2`, so it is allowed by the final-source test.
Weighted multiplicity does not change this yes/no domain membership. ∎

Thus direct allocation is path-independent even though the dynamic potential is
not: the potential records intermediate incidences, while the final domains do
not.

## 3. Final binary support depends only on surviving points

The final new point set is row/column-disjoint in every endpoint, rectangle,
cycle, and filler trade considered here.  Since `S_2` is no-three-in-line, every
pair in `N_2` determines a distinct nonaxis secant.

### Proposition PP3ahx -- PROVED

The complete final binary candidate support satisfies

```text
Delta(Z_bin^M(N_2)) <= binom(s_f,2),
Delta(Z_bin^F(N_2)) <= binom(s_f,2)
```

on both the controller-edge and final-label sides.  Consequently one paired
macro domain loses at most

```text
2 binom(s_f,2)=s_f(s_f-1)
```

values to all final binary insertion shadow.

#### Proof

Apply PP3aha--PP3ahc to the final inserted set `N_2`.  The proof uses only the
final source-validity and matching properties of `N_2`, not the path by which
those points were installed. ∎

In particular a very large binary `Xi` cost in `S_1` is irrelevant when all of
its supporting first-step points are removed before final allocation.

## 4. Direct final allocation after two trades

Let

```text
H_i^base(A,B)
```

be the final macro domain after testing the retained original source `O_2` and
all source, anchor, transition, and non-`Xi` exclusions.  Let

```text
H_i^final(A,B)
```

also delete the unary and binary candidate entries generated by `N_2`.

For the final unary support, use the degree notation of PP3ahj:

```text
d_M^final(i,A),
d_F^final(i,B).
```

### Theorem PP3ahy -- PROVED / CONDITIONAL DIRECT-COMPLETION INTERFACE

Fix constants `gamma,xi>0`.  Assume:

1. both trades are source-admissible and preserve the controller infrastructure;
2. all nonfinal-shadow exclusions are included in `H_i^base(A,B)`;
3. the margin graphs

   ```text
   J_i^base(gamma+xi)
   ```

   satisfy PP3fy or PP3gl;
4. the local width condition in PP3ho holds;
5. for every macro and final label pair,

   ```text
   d_M^final(i,A)+d_F^final(i,B)+s_f(s_f-1) <= xi R.
   ```

Then

```text
J_i^base(gamma+xi) subseteq J_i^final(gamma)
```

for every macro.  The same balanced ownership and global label matching survive,
and PP3hq produces the final saturated no-three patch.

No bound is required on the intermediate insertion cost in `S_1`, and neither
trade needs to decrease `Xi`.

#### Proof

PP3ahw removes every transient entry.  PP3ahj applied to the final pair
`(O_2,N_2)`, together with PP3ahx, bounds the loss from each margin-compatible
domain by `xi R`.  The post-shadow domain therefore has size at least `gamma R`.
Retain the same global allocation and apply PP3ho--PP3hq. ∎

A sufficient label-free condition is

```text
U_Xi^final+s_f(s_f-1) <= xi R,
```

where `U_Xi^final` is the unary incidence weight of final new--old blocker pairs.

## 5. Failure creates a final-state source star

Suppose the binary term fits the reserved margin but final unary support pushes
one base domain below threshold.  Assign each lost value to a causing final new
point and to movement or refill type.

### Corollary PP3ahz -- PROVED

If a domain of initial size at least `(gamma+xi)R` falls below `gamma R`, then
some point `a in N_2` is the common source endpoint of a final unary fibre of
size

```text
C > xi R/(2s_f).
```

The fibre has distinct controller candidate entries and distinct retained old
partners.  Hence the final source contains a genuine next-generation source
star centred at `a`.

#### Proof

This is PP3aho--PP3ahp applied to the final source decomposition
`S_2=O_2 dot-union N_2`.  More than `xi R` unary deletions split among at most
`2s_f` point/type classes. ∎

The star is supported by the final source, not by an intermediate accounting
state, so it is a valid input to the marked source-star trade PP3agh--PP3agn.

## 6. Revised two-step endpoint

### Corollary PP3aia -- PROVED

A two-step source-valid controller-preserving trade path has one of the following
forms.

1. The final unary and binary support fits the robust base-domain margin, and the
   patch completes directly by PP3ahy.
2. Final unary support destroys a domain margin and yields a source star of
   degree `Omega(R/s_f)` by PP3ahz.
3. The base nonshadow margin or global allocation criterion fails.
4. A source, transition, anchor, conditional-Hall, alternating-host, or endpoint
   obstruction prevents selection of the two source-valid trades.
5. The final new point set is too large for its support to fit the available
   margin.

Arbitrary first-step or second-step foreign `Xi` multiplicity is not an
independent obstruction in the robust direct-allocation branch.  Only final
simple support matters.  Potential descent remains necessary in branches where
no robust final allocation is available.

## 7. Finite diagnostic

The script

```text
scripts/check_final_state_shadow_path.py
```

constructs a sequence of source-valid trades, computes the simple candidate
support after every stage, verifies that transient-only entries disappear from
the final support, and tests the exact final macro-domain loss against the unary
plus binary bound.
