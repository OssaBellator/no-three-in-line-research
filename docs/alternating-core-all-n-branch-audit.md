# Interface audit of the three all-n research branches

**Branch:** `research/alternating-core-chain`

This note records the status of the three independent all-`n` branches as of 25 July 2026 and identifies which interfaces can be imported into the alternating-core chain without strengthening their hypotheses.

## Prime patching

`research/all-n-prime-patching` is the most developed asymptotic patch route. Its degree-restoration interface is proved, and its matching/selection results are proved as implications. The slab-optimal architecture, controller-aware source safety, canonical ownership deficiency, Hall-rectangle extraction, superregular rectangle installation, conditional binary stars, paid choice grids, non-superregular SCC factorization and fixed-centre petal banks are serialized.

The branch remains open at concentrated conversion cores:

- controller denominator or local Ore slack failure;
- source-star/resource-bank and fixed-centre arc/path-petal payment;
- chromatically concentrated unary/binary shadow weight;
- centre-core/local-credit concentration;
- alternating cycle-star or theta cost at the available credit scale;
- quadratic binary fans, choice grids, projective covers and conditional Hall residuals.

Its SCC mobility, cycle-star and theta-state theorems supplied the matching geometry used in AC3ig--AC3ip. The import is deliberately limited:

- directed Menger gives a one-hub star or two-hub separator;
- cycle and theta states are legal equal-margin alternatives in one fixed host;
- exact cylinder multiplicities are finite support counts.

The prime-patching controller, source-validity and asymptotic credit hypotheses are not imported. Alternating core instead uses its independently proved private current pivot bucket. This is why AC3il--AC3ip can execute arbitrary overlapping static cycle families without claiming the prime patch.

## Composite modulus

`research/all-n-composite-modulus` has the deepest exact temporal matching ledger. The live range reaches CMR545 and includes:

- monotone-mask selected-state cycle erasure;
- exact returned-edge incidence for distinct matching states;
- polynomial signature stocks inside nested prime-power envelope epochs;
- deletion/forced-ancestry dichotomies;
- essential-core exchange corridors and rollback factorization;
- line-clean pair cylinders, unavailable-edge absorption and temporal pair/trace ancestry.

The live prime-power frontier is repeated failure of one fixed compatible-pair line-clean selector without double counting across envelope epochs. Arbitrary side-length coverage remains open afterward.

AC3hx imports only the general monotone-mask state-cycle principle. AC3hy--AC3ia reprove the alternating-core-specific cross signature, ticket and long-cycle outputs directly. Prime-power token, rollback, ancestry and envelope statements are not imported without their arithmetic hypotheses.

## Product construction

`research/all-n-product-construction` proves:

- saturated four-orientation product encodings;
- exact mixed-radix determinant and carry classifications;
- factor-host line and pair-codegree bounds;
- width-three CNF formulations for phase and full degree-two host selection;
- alternating-cycle connectivity of product-compatible states;
- explicit saturated witnesses at sides `6`, `8` and `9`.

It also records decisive counterexamples: independent global orientation/cycle phases do not always suffice, and neither raw triple count nor triple count plus pair-line energy is a monotone repair potential. Its remaining route needs a global selector or simultaneous hybrid-determinant concentration. It currently supplies no stronger general recurrence interface than AC3hx--AC3ip.

## Current cross-branch composition

The safe composition map is:

1. use alternating core to obtain prime-minus-one saturated states under AC4/AC5;
2. use prime patching only after its remaining concentrated conversion theorem supplies a saturation-compatible controller allocation;
3. use composite-modulus results for prime-power envelopes only where prefix, token, rollback and ancestry hypotheses are physically realized;
4. retain product construction as an independent finite/product-host route, not as a substitute for AC4 termination.

The immediate alternating-core gains from the audit are now exact:

- AC3hx: monotone-mask selected-state cycle erasure;
- AC3hy--AC3ia: finite cross signatures and rectangle/long-cycle routing;
- AC3ib--AC3if: path-petal and common-parent petal structure;
- AC3ig--AC3ik: finite return-cross ticket stock and repeated long-return routing;

None of the three all-`n` branches currently proves the conjecture.
