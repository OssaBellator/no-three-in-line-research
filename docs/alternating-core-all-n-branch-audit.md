# Interface audit of the three all-n research branches

**Branch:** `research/alternating-core-chain`

This note records the status of the three independent all-`n` branches as of
25 July 2026 and identifies which interfaces can be imported into the
alternating-core chain without strengthening their hypotheses.

## Prime patching

`research/all-n-prime-patching` is the most developed asymptotic patch route.
Its degree-restoration interface is proved, and its matching/selection results
are proved as implications. The slab-optimal architecture, controller-aware
source safety, canonical ownership deficiency, Hall-rectangle extraction,
superregular rectangle installation, conditional binary stars, paid choice
grids, non-superregular SCC factorization and fixed-centre petal banks are all
serialized.

The branch remains open at concentrated conversion cores:

- controller denominator or local Ore slack failure;
- source-star/resource-bank and fixed-centre arc/path-petal payment;
- chromatically concentrated unary/binary shadow weight;
- centre-core/local-credit concentration;
- alternating cycle-star or theta cost at the available credit scale;
- quadratic binary fans, choice grids, projective covers and conditional Hall
  residuals.

Its SCC mobility theorems are relevant to AC3ia's fixed-cross long alternating
cycle, but they require the prime-patching credited-host and source-validity
hypotheses. No unconditional AC import is made.

## Composite modulus

`research/all-n-composite-modulus` has the deepest exact temporal matching
ledger. The live range reaches CMR545 and includes:

- monotone-mask selected-state cycle erasure;
- exact returned-edge incidence for distinct matching states;
- polynomial signature stocks inside nested prime-power envelope epochs;
- deletion/forced-ancestry dichotomies;
- essential-core exchange corridors and rollback factorization;
- line-clean pair cylinders, unavailable-edge absorption and temporal
  pair/trace ancestry.

The live prime-power frontier is repeated failure of one fixed compatible-pair
line-clean selector without double counting across envelope epochs. Arbitrary
side-length coverage remains open afterward.

AC3hx imports only the general monotone-mask state-cycle principle. AC3hy--AC3ia
reprove the alternating-core-specific cross signature, ticket and long-cycle
outputs directly. Prime-power token, ancestry and envelope claims are not
imported without their arithmetic hypotheses.

## Product construction

`research/all-n-product-construction` proves:

- saturated four-orientation product encodings;
- exact mixed-radix determinant and carry classifications;
- factor-host line and pair-codegree bounds;
- width-three CNF formulations for phase and full degree-two host selection;
- alternating-cycle connectivity of product-compatible states;
- explicit saturated witnesses at sides `6`, `8` and `9`.

It also records decisive counterexamples: independent global orientation/cycle
phases do not always suffice, and neither raw triple count nor triple count plus
pair-line energy is a monotone repair potential. Its remaining route needs a
global selector or simultaneous hybrid-determinant concentration. It currently
supplies no stronger general recurrence interface than AC3hx--AC3ia.

## Current cross-branch composition

The safe composition map is therefore:

1. use the alternating core to obtain prime-minus-one saturated states under
   AC4/AC5;
2. use prime patching only after its remaining concentrated conversion theorem
   supplies a saturation-compatible controller allocation;
3. use composite-modulus results for prime-power envelopes only where their
   prefix, token and ancestry hypotheses are physically realized;
4. retain product construction as an independent finite/product-host route, not
   as a substitute for AC4 termination.

None of the three all-`n` branches currently proves the conjecture. The immediate
alternating-core benefit from the audit is the exact state-cycle and
cross-signature router AC3hx--AC3ia.
