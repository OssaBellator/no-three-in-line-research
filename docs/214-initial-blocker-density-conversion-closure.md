# Initial movement/refill blocker-density conversion closure

The initial controller-aware allocation theorem can fail because many movement or
refill candidate entries have a noncontroller blocker pair.  PP3hx--PP3hy show
that positive-density failure already contains either a large source star or a
prime-gap-scale resource matching.  The later marked-star, recapture-free bank,
final-state, and controller-puncture theorems now convert both alternatives to the
same explicit interfaces.

The conclusion is not that the initial allocation theorem is proved.  It is that
positive-density movement/refill blocker shadow is no longer an unstructured
frontier: it must pay, complete directly, expose a prepared-host/support core, or
consume a controller puncture reserve.

## 1. Structured extraction at the slab scale

Let

```text
M=m^(1/20+o(1)),
R=m^(19/20+o(1)),
W=m^(19/40+o(1)),
T=MW=m^(21/40+o(1)).
```

There are `2MTR` typed movement/refill controller entries.  Assume at least a
fixed `delta>0` fraction are bad and choose one noncontroller blocker pair for
each bad entry.

### Proposition PP3akq -- PROVED FROM PP3hx--PP3hy

At least one of the following holds.

1. **Source-star branch.** One source point is a blocker endpoint in
   `Omega(m)` bad entries and has at least

   ```text
   Omega(W)
   ```

   distinct blocker partners.
2. **Full resource-bank branch.** There are

   ```text
   Omega_delta(T)
   ```

   bad entries with distinct typed labels, distinct controllers, pairwise
   endpoint-disjoint blocker pairs, and no controller/blocker resource overlap.

#### Proof

This is exactly the resource-hypergraph dichotomy PP3hx and its slab-scale
corollary PP3hy. ∎

The first alternative is stronger in entry multiplicity than the basic
star-or-matching theorem; the second already has all candidate resources needed
for endpoint-bank conversion.

## 2. Complete star conversion interface

### Theorem PP3akr -- PROVED / CONDITIONAL MARKED-HOST INTERFACE

Every source star from PP3akq has one of the following conversions.

1. If its centre is outside the active controller infrastructure, it is a free
   marked centre and feeds PP3agh--PP3agn together with the fresh-helper cascade
   PP3ais--PP3aje.
2. If its centre is an active controller, remove that one value from its pool.
   By PP3akg every designated star entry remains in the punctured universe, and
   the centre becomes free at one-value domain cost.
3. Alternatively, PP3ajy--PP3akd gives a controller-preserving free-partner bank
   unless at least half the partners are controllers.
4. A source-valid punctured or partner-bank state then gives a paid improvement,
   robust direct completion, a new free super-target star, or an explicit
   source/transition/anchor/Hall/alternating/endpoint-host or foreign-support
   obstruction.

#### Proof

Free centres use the marked source-star theorem.  Captive centres use the
one-controller puncture theorem PP3ake--PP3akj or the partner split
PP3ajy--PP3akd.  The paid and direct endpoints are PP3akh--PP3aki. ∎

Thus no star centre remains geometrically captive.  Repeated punctures are
accounted for by the reserve process PP3akk--PP3akp.

## 3. Complete resource-bank conversion interface

Take the full resource matching from PP3akq.  Its endpoint-disjoint blocker pairs
carry distinct designated candidate incidences and distinct controller resources.
Choose a common blocker layer as in PP3hz and then a large tied endpoint bank.

### Theorem PP3aks -- PROVED / CONDITIONAL ENDPOINT-HOST INTERFACE

The resource-bank branch has one of the following outcomes.

1. Random thinning produces a source-regular selected bank with zero designated
   credit-line self-recapture, as in PP3afn--PP3aft.
2. A source-valid endpoint derangement strictly decreases the dynamic
   controller-shadow potential.
3. Adaptive ambient thinning leaves a positive-density unary, rank-three, or
   rank-four foreign support star.
4. The selected state completes through final-state direct allocation.
5. Source, transition, anchor, Hall, alternating, or endpoint-host preparation
   fails explicitly.

#### Proof

Apply the layer refinement PP3hz, the credited-line self-recapture theorem
PP3afn--PP3aft, the recapture-free endpoint PP3afu--PP3afz, and adaptive ambient
support thinning PP3aga--PP3agg.  The robust alternative is the final-state
allocation chain PP3ahv--PP3ajx. ∎

Diffuse self-recapture and diffuse foreign support are not additional cases.

## 4. Positive-density blocker endpoint

### Theorem PP3akt -- PROVED / CONDITIONAL COMBINED CONVERSION INTERFACE

Suppose a fixed positive fraction of the initial movement/refill controller
entries is bad.  Then at least one of the following occurs.

1. A strict paid controller-shadow improvement.
2. Robust direct completion.
3. A new free source star of size at least the target width, and in the adaptive
   direct branch of size `omega(W)`.
4. A positive-density ambient unary, rank-three, or rank-four foreign support
   core.
5. A marked source, transition, anchor, Hall, alternating, distinguished
   endpoint, or resource-bank host obstruction.
6. A macro-local controller-puncture history reaching the prescribed reserve.
7. Failure of the initial nonshadow ownership/global-allocation certificate.

#### Proof

Apply PP3akq.  Convert the star alternative with PP3akr and the resource-bank
alternative with PP3aks.  Repeated captive star use is governed by
PP3akk--PP3akp. ∎

The theorem is intentionally modular: every remaining case is already expressed
at the exact interface required by the paid, robust, or host-preparation parts of
the proof.

## 5. Revised initial allocation frontier

### Corollary PP3aku -- PROVED

A positive-density movement/refill blocker shadow with no source structure is
impossible.  After the conversion chains above, the initial controller-aware
allocation theorem is reduced to:

1. closing the explicit marked/endpoint-host and foreign-support alternatives;
2. same-slot anchor concentration;
3. ownership-Hall deficiency and capped refill concentration;
4. macro excess-shadow concentration requiring one-step paid descent;
5. controller-puncture reserve exhaustion;
6. the `o(1)` blocker-density regime, where complementary-degree allocation must
   be proved quantitatively rather than by positive-density extraction.

In particular, a free star, captive star, controller--controller star, and
resource-disjoint blocker bank are no longer separate initial geometric
frontiers.

No completion of the no-three-in-line conjecture is claimed.
