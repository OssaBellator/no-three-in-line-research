# Geometric-cleaning height-deposit source conservation

This note records GC2ih--GC2il. It gives an occurrence-faithful source account for replenishing the clean-height reserve.

## Contract

Height-credit tokens occupy a finite exact source dictionary. Initial tokens are exogenous. Every legal internal transition moves one token from one source address to another or deposits that token into the clean-height reserve. No transition splits a token, increases its weight, or creates a token without a predecessor. Deposited reserve units retain their source lineage.

## Theorem block

### GC2ih — source-plus-reserve conservation

At every time,

`live source credit + cumulative deposited reserve credit`

is at most the initial exogenous height credit. Under exact one-for-one transitions it is equal before reserve spending.

### GC2ii — deposit bound

The cumulative clean-height deposits generated by the source system are at most the initial exogenous credit.

### GC2ij — reserve-bank composition

Combining GC2ii with the clean-height reserve bank, cumulative paid clean-height loss is bounded by initial reserve plus initial exogenous source credit.

### GC2ik — overload alternative

The first loss exceeding the remaining reserve returns an exact height overload. Splitting, weight increase or source-less creation returns an exact amplification obstruction instead of free replenishment.

### GC2il — reset boundary

Omitted source identity, changed credit weight, duplicated lineage, unrecorded exogenous input or a changing source dictionary is outside the theorem and returns reset.

## Proof

Each legal transition preserves the sum of live source units and deposited reserve units. Reserve spending only decreases the total. The remaining statements follow by composition with the clean-height bank.

## Remaining physical work

The theorem does not prove that the geometric cleaning moves admit this one-for-one height-credit lineage, nor does it bound the initial physical source stock.
