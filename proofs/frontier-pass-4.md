## Updated AC4 frontier

Buffer-rich positive-output circulations have the upstream AC3ur--AC3uv payment route. Low-buffer exact returns reduce to strict resource-dimension descent, first-destruction/source payment, threshold tickets or recreation gates; finite occurrence-faithful recreation gates reduce further to source debit, capacity-one restoration tickets, reset or impossibility. Bounded recurrent words that use finite upper guards give finite exact guarded-coordinate decorations; full guarding is ticketed, while partial guarding strictly lowers the number of unbounded additive coordinates. Hidden rational linear balances with recurrent lower and upper guards lift to finite exact balance coordinates; full balance rank is finite and partial rank descends to the Smith-kernel dimension. One-sided translation-complete additive tails normalize to a finite prefix shape plus one base counter. Multiple one-sided counters are also reduced whenever their guard dependence is triangular: the first nonzero drift coordinate either escapes monotonically or exhausts a finite exact headroom budget.

The remaining recurrent cases require genuinely fresh or cyclically replenished physical sources, unbounded atom sets or thresholds, genuinely nonlinear or nonadditive legality, cyclic rather than triangular counter dependence, increments depending on absolute balance, dynamic dictionaries, or payment-sensitive fields omitted from the complete boundary state.

## Updated AC5 frontier

The deterministic GC1--GC3 inventory now has a closed-form restricted-menu audit. For retained pool size `p_*`, maximum exact blocker/new-blocker load `Delta` and batch size `t`, positive slack

`sigma=p_*-Delta-t`

gives a `sigma^(-r)` cylinder law. The cumulative new-assignment counts feed explicit intermediate current/protected expectations, and the AC5p inequality certifies one descending installation path. Nonpositive slack returns a heavy physical-support, dangerous-line, residual-context, new-blocker or batch-size obstruction instead of discarding the menu.

The remaining work is menu-specific: compute `c,Delta,A_j,Theta_(J,0),ell_J`, pay the returned heavy obstruction when slack fails, and establish the resulting drift or reverse-flow/min-cost inequality through all intermediate states.

No statement here proves AC4, AC5, AC6 or the no-three-in-line conjecture.
