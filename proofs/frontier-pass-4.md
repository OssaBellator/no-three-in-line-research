## Updated AC5 frontier

The deterministic GC1--GC3 inventory now has a closed-form restricted-menu audit. For retained pool size `p_*`, maximum exact blocker/new-blocker load `Delta` and batch size `t`, positive slack

`sigma=p_*-Delta-t`

gives a `sigma^(-r)` cylinder law. The cumulative new-assignment counts feed explicit intermediate current/protected expectations, and the AC5p inequality certifies one descending installation path. Nonpositive slack returns a heavy physical-support, dangerous-line, residual-context, new-blocker or batch-size obstruction instead of discarding the menu.

The remaining work is menu-specific: compute `c,Delta,A_j,Theta_(J,0),ell_J`, pay the returned heavy obstruction when slack fails, and establish the resulting drift or reverse-flow/min-cost inequality through all intermediate states.
