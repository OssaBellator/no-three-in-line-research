## Updated AC4 frontier

Buffer-rich positive-output circulations have the upstream AC3ur--AC3uv payment route. Low-buffer returns, recreation cycles, guarded coordinates, rational balances and one-sided additive tails now have finite or monotone routers. Multiple counters are covered under triangular dependence, finite residue phases, fixed absolute-threshold tails and moving thresholds whose relative dynamics is finite-phase. The remaining recurrent cases require genuinely fresh or cyclically replenished physical sources, unbounded atom/control dictionaries, nonlinear or nonadditive legality, tail rules depending arbitrarily on unbounded magnitude beyond a co-moving finite phase, or payment-sensitive fields omitted from the complete state.

The deterministic GC1--GC3 inventory now has a closed-form restricted-menu audit, a finite exact obstruction-capacity bank, exact reverse-load transportation, Hall-core obstruction routing, finite-layer aggregation, local bad-layer concentration and a classwise weighted shortfall bank.
