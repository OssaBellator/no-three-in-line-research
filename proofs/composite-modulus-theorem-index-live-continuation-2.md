The branch still does not prove the all-`n` conjecture. Completeness branching
can now be made disjoint. At one target-resolution stage, all terminal leaves
compress to at most

\[
8\binom{n^2}{3}
\]

fixed labelled-triple set-family classes. Unions of leaves with different masks
are not assumed to be one common matching-host family.

For minimum-potential arguments, a different normal form avoids branch width.
Choose an actual minimum state. Deleting edges outside that anchor cannot discard
a better state and forces a selected target or anchor prescription after at most
`2n^2-2n` deletions. Exact contraction preserves minimality for the induced
residual objective.

At one owner, every edge is either omitted by some minimum state and is therefore
minimum-preservingly deletable, or belongs to the complete minimum core and
contracts. Along the finite physical-edge owner lineage, each owner slot receives
at most one uncharged first closure. Every later active appearance is a genuine
restoration unless the edge contracts. Thus the former fixed-owner, fixed-edge
recurrence has deletion, contraction, finite owner stock, or exact full-token
payment.

The active prime-power frontier is now dynamic owner transition and return
capacity: control how minimum faces and their common cores change when the owner,
host, factor, wall, or envelope changes, and convert the accumulated full-token
incidence into inherited target-load decrease, reserve exhaustion, or strict
global potential improvement. Prime-field transfer and arbitrary side-length
assembly remain necessary afterward.
