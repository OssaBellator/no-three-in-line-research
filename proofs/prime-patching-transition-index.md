## Current transition endpoint

A fixed captive-centre transition obstruction now yields either:

1. a paid clean-chain completion;
2. source or `Xi` concentration at the clean-chain credit scale;
3. a free credited endpoint bank of the marked target size; or
4. a credited endpoint bank of the marked target size inside one controller pool.

The transition sunflower is therefore not an independent frontier. It rejoins
the existing paid source-star/resource-bank conversion problem. The finite
bookkeeping diagnostic is
