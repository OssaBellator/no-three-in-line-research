## Logical gain

The previous AC consolidation produced a finite-resource episode bound but still lacked one strict macro-state descent mechanism. AC5hu and AC5hw supply that mechanism:

1. inside a fixed repair universe, canonical minimax moves strictly lower a finite rank;
2. when the active menu or controller pairing changes, zero-mass activation prevents the restart potential from increasing;
3. supply, paid restart and minimax repair become the three levels of one mixed-radix scalar potential.

Semantic coverage is kept fail-closed: AC may use a compiled evaluator only on the exact covered union, and every uncovered tagged pair is a named frontier witness.
