# Frontier pass: RI predicate-independent stocks

Head theorem block: RI5hi--RI5hl.

- Dense named predicate mass is retained as an edge graph.
- Bounded endpoint degree gives a matching of size at least `ceil(e/(2D-1))`; otherwise a degree-`D+1` star is explicit.
- Bounded physical-witness multiplicity gives an endpoint- and witness-distinct stock of size at least `ceil(|M|/mu)`; otherwise one witness is overloaded.
- Under a per-witness payment contract, the stock is capacity-one payable.

Remaining frontier: construct the physical predicate/witness dictionary and prove useful degree, multiplicity and payment-efficiency bounds.
