# Frontier pass: AC predicate-independent stocks

Head theorem block: AC5gl--AC5go.

- A synchronized deficit concentrates on one track-support-key triple and one named predicate with edge mass at least `m^2/q_s`.
- Bounded endpoint degree gives a within-track matching of size at least `ceil(e/(2D-1))`; otherwise a typed high-degree failure star is explicit.
- Bounded physical-witness multiplicity gives an incidence-, token- and witness-distinct stock of size at least `ceil(|N|/mu)`; otherwise one typed witness is overloaded.
- Under track payment efficiency `rho_s`, the stock is capacity-one payable without cross-ledger cancellation.

Remaining frontier: construct the physical witness dictionaries and prove useful degree, multiplicity and payment constants; discharge the star and overload branches in AC6.
