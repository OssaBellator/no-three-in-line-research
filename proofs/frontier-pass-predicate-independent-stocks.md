# Frontier pass: GC predicate-independent stocks

Head theorem block: GC2mp--GC2ms.

- A named geometric predicate retains at least `m^2/q` failed remedy-donor pairs.
- Bounded endpoint degree gives a matching of size at least `ceil(e/(2D-1))`; otherwise a high-degree geometric star is explicit.
- Bounded physical-witness multiplicity yields a remedy-, donor- and witness-distinct stock of size at least `ceil(|M|/mu)`; otherwise one chart/protected-event witness is overloaded.
- Under per-witness efficiency `rho`, the stock is capacity-one payable.

Remaining frontier: construct the physical geometric witness dictionary and prove concrete degree, multiplicity and payment bounds.
