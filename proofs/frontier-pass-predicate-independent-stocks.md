# Frontier pass: BDA predicate-independent stocks

Head theorem block: BDA5hr--BDA5hu.

- The named restoration predicate retains at least `m^2/q` failed pairs.
- Bounded endpoint degree gives a matching of size at least `ceil(e/(2D-1))`; otherwise an explicit failed-predicate star is returned.
- Bounded physical-witness multiplicity yields an endpoint- and witness-distinct stock of size at least `ceil(|M|/mu)`; otherwise one restoration witness is overloaded.
- Under per-witness efficiency `rho`, the stock is capacity-one payable.

Remaining frontier: construct the restoration witness dictionary and prove concrete degree, multiplicity and payment bounds.
