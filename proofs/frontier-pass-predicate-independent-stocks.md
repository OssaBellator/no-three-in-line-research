# Frontier pass: SRR predicate-independent stocks

Head theorem block: SRR2fy--SRR2gb.

- A named conditioned predicate retains at least `m^2/q` failed candidate-witness pairs.
- Bounded endpoint degree gives a matching of size at least `ceil(e/(2D-1))`; otherwise a high-degree conditioned star is explicit.
- Bounded physical-witness multiplicity yields a candidate-, witness-token- and physical-witness-distinct stock of size at least `ceil(|M|/mu)`; otherwise one cycle/threshold/burden witness is overloaded.
- Under per-witness efficiency `rho`, the stock is capacity-one payable.

Remaining frontier: construct the physical conditioned witness dictionary and prove concrete degree, multiplicity and payment bounds.
