# Frontier pass: SAS predicate-independent stocks

Head theorem block: SAS5no--SAS5nr.

- A named sparse predicate retains at least `m^2/q` failed pair/completion-neutral pairs.
- Bounded endpoint degree gives a matching of size at least `ceil(e/(2D-1))`; otherwise a high-degree sparse star is explicit.
- Bounded physical-witness multiplicity yields a pair/completion-, neutral-token- and witness-distinct stock of size at least `ceil(|M|/mu)`; otherwise one move/legality/orientation witness is overloaded.
- Under per-witness efficiency `rho`, the stock is capacity-one payable.

Remaining frontier: construct the physical sparse witness dictionary and prove concrete degree, multiplicity and payment bounds.
