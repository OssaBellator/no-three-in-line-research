# Frontier pass: OP predicate-independent stocks

Head theorem block: OP4go--OP4gr.

- A named phase predicate retains at least `m^2/q` failed residual/edit-source pairs.
- Bounded endpoint degree gives a matching of size at least `ceil(e/(2D-1))`; otherwise a high-degree phase star is explicit.
- Bounded phase-witness multiplicity yields a residual/edit-, source- and witness-distinct stock of size at least `ceil(|M|/mu)`; otherwise one quotient/unit/holonomy witness is overloaded.
- Under per-witness efficiency `rho`, the stock is capacity-one payable.

Remaining frontier: construct the physical phase witness dictionary and prove concrete degree, multiplicity and payment bounds.
