# Frontier pass: rational fixed-ratio capacity

- GC4bc turns a fixed ratio `lambda=alpha` into the polynomial equation `f-alpha g=0` and isolates the constant-ratio case.
- GC4bd gives incidence `DM` for every nonconstant fixed-ratio address and `(deg g)M` for denominator exceptions.
- GC4be sums these capacities over a finite ratio dictionary.
- GC4bf returns either root-bounded ratio addresses, one constant homothetic family, identical cells, bounded denominator exceptions or a precise record failure.

The remaining GC5 work is to pay constant-ratio families, construct actual ratio dictionaries and control eligible top weights.
