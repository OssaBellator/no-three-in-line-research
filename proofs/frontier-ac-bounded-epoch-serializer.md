## Logical gain

The atlas census previously assumed that one complete bounded physical object universe `Omega sqcup V sqcup E` had already been supplied. The new serializer makes that assumption finite and auditable.

For one fixed target batch, retained partner pool, finite job catalogue, recreation-atom set, controller dictionary, tail cap, resource caps and globally addressed capacity caps, the ambient state count is

\[
N_{\rm box}
=
\left(\sum_{j=0}^{t}(p_*)_j\right)
\left(\sum_{k=0}^{|J|}(|J|)_k\right)
2^{|A|}F(H+1)
\prod_i(K_i+1)
\prod_a(C_a+1).
\]

Every state-operation pair has exactly one typed result. Internal outcomes form `E_box`; noninternal outcomes retain exact shortage, amplification, overflow, monotone-escape or reset records in `partial_box`.

For nested caps, old internal objects are unchanged. An old upper-bound record either remains a boundary object or refines to its exact internal successor. The new ambient shell has size

\[
N_{\rm shell}=N_{\rm box}'-N_{\rm box}.
\]

Thus cap enlargement is monotone proof reuse rather than a fresh epoch proof.
