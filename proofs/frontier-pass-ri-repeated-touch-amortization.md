# Frontier pass: RI repeated-touch amortization

## New theorem block

- RI5ic: exact interruption decomposition.
- RI5id: finite rank-reset amortization.
- RI5ie: bounded service or accumulated RI payment.
- RI5if: queue-wide waiting envelope funded by payment, initial deficit, and support-local churn.

## Advance

A persistently interrupted RI repair can no longer remain an unstructured repeated-touch certificate. Every blocked service window is occurrence-faithfully routed to one named payment, one integer-rank descent, one funded reset, or one exact unclassified-touch failure. Finite rank and reset allowances force all excess interruption into the payment ledger.

## Remaining RI6 work

Construct the arithmetic primitive ranks, reset sources, and paid-touch ledgers; prove uniform structural allowance `Q_*`; and show paid touches force collateral payment, geometric descent, or a finite macro-state change.
