# Frontier pass: BDA repeated-touch amortization

## New theorem block

- BDA5il: exact restoration-interruption decomposition.
- BDA5im: finite restoration rank-reset allowance.
- BDA5in: bounded service or accumulated typed payment.
- BDA5io: queue-wide restoration waiting envelope.

## Advance

Repeated restoration edits now have only four legal outcomes: typed payment, strict primitive-rank descent, funded reset consumption, or an exact unclassified-touch failure. Finite rank and reset allowances force all excess waiting into the rational-gain payment ledger.

## Remaining BDA6 work

Construct concrete restoration ranks, reset tokens, and payment ledgers; prove a uniform structural allowance; and convert paid touches into gain, exhaustion, reset, or monotone descent.
