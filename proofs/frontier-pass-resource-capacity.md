# Frontier pass: conflict-resource capacity

- RI5cd proves the exact partition of a heavy labeled conflict class by least physical payment resource.
- RI5ce extracts a resource-disjoint maximum-representative stock retaining at least a `1/mu` fraction under resource multiplicity `mu`.
- RI5cf gives the high-resource-star versus disjoint-paying-stock dichotomy.
- RI5cg composes this with the failed-bank and finite-label bounds, retaining at least `W/((q-1)(K+1)R mu)`.

The remaining RI6 work is to establish class-specific resource multiplicity and payment efficiencies, and to close repeated-coset, blocker and recurrence obligations.
