one type repeats, one pair cell deletes matching-preservingly or both essential
pair cells contract, reducing factor side by two and leaving a rank-one trigger.

restored owner edges after these deletion/contraction responses, against full-

budget. Prime-field transfer and arbitrary side-length assembly remain necessary
