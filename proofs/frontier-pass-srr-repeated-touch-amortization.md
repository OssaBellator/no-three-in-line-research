# Frontier pass: SRR repeated-touch amortization

## New theorem block

- SRR2gs: exact conditioned-interruption decomposition.
- SRR2gt: finite conditioned rank-reset allowance.
- SRR2gu: bounded service or accumulated conditioned payment.
- SRR2gv: queue-wide conditioned waiting envelope.

## Advance

Repeated resampling interruption must now pay a typed conditioned ledger, strictly decrease a primitive rank, consume a funded reset, or expose an exact invalid touch. Finite structural allowance forces excess waiting into source, blocker, threshold, or burden payment.

## Remaining SRR work

Construct concrete conditioned ranks, reset sources, and paid-touch ledgers; prove a uniform allowance; and convert accumulated payment into endpoint-burden descent, reset, or finite recurrence progress.
