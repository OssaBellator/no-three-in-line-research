# Frontier pass: SAS repeated-touch amortization

## New theorem block

- SAS5oi: exact sparse-interruption decomposition.
- SAS5oj: finite sparse rank-reset allowance.
- SAS5ok: bounded service or accumulated sparse payment.
- SAS5ol: queue-wide sparse waiting envelope.

## Advance

Repeated sparse interruption must now pay a typed neutral/orientation/lineage ledger, strictly decrease a primitive rank, consume a funded reset, or expose an exact invalid touch. Finite structural allowance forces excess waiting into explicit sparse payment.

## Remaining SAS6 work

Construct concrete sparse ranks, reset sources, and paid-touch ledgers; prove a uniform allowance; and convert accumulated payment into neutral/orientation/lineage descent, reset, or finite recurrence progress.
