## Updated frontier

The branch has exact Hall-core causes, clean-height costs, integrated cuts, pressure/path/segment localization, occurrence-slot lineage and assignment Hall cores. Remaining GC5 work is constructing the concrete donor/remedy/height use/slot graph and proving every returned maximum-deficit cause core has enough faithful slots, is paid by named deposits or is geometrically impossible, including untagged feedback and local resampling.

No statement here proves GC5 or the no-three-in-line conjecture.
