- **GC4aq:** coefficient degeneracy is exactly `c_0=c_1=c_2=0`; otherwise there are at most two roots.
- **GC4ar:** physical parameter multiplicity `M_p` gives incidence at most `2M_p` and top-weight capacity `S_p(2M_p)`.
- **GC4as:** every affine line event returns a degree-two capacity or one exact coefficient/fibre defect.
