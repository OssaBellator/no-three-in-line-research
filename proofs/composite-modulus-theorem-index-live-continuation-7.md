canonical source registry. Coverage and source use are checked in both directions. This
proves traceability only; it does not prove that the cited statements are true or jointly
exhaustive.

## Simultaneous credit nonreuse

assignments. A global destroyed-resource key is determined by resource scope and literal

witness. Both key families must be injective over the declared simultaneous execution.

The scope and instance partition remains supplied data and still needs mathematical

## Auxiliary elimination

Every positively used auxiliary state `a` receives one one-step nonauxiliary expansion
with

\[
f_a+\sum_t m_{a,t}w_t\le w_a.
\]

No credit may be routed to an eliminated auxiliary coordinate. Responsewise substitution
therefore gives

\[
L_{\rm elim}(Q)\le L(Q),
\qquad
\mu_{\rm elim}(Q)\ge\mu(Q).
\]

This proves weighted domination of the declared expansion, not its external transition
semantics.
