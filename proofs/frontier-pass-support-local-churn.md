# SAS frontier pass: support-local neutral churn

- SAS5ng: every changed sparse record has a least touched primitive dependency, or returns a nonlocal sparse-change or lineage witness.
- SAS5nh: support size `H` and primitive incidence caps bound changed pair/completion and neutral records by `H alpha_R`, `H alpha_+`, and `H alpha_-`.
- SAS5ni: audit cost is at most `H(alpha_R L_T+alpha_+ L_R)` and neutral-assignment disturbance at most `H(alpha_R+alpha_-)`.
- SAS5nj: a residual deficit `delta` has only support-caused roots; one touched move primitive anchors at least `ceil(delta/H)` roots.

Validation: 2,500 systems, 57,215 key-local pairs versus 234,059 full-product pairs, 16,217 disturbed roots and 10,777 residual deficits.
