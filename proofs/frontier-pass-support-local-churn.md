- GC2mi: support size `H` and primitive incidence caps bound changed remedy and donor records by `H alpha_R`, `H alpha_+`, and `H alpha_-`.
- GC2mj: audit cost is at most `H(alpha_R L_T+alpha_+ L_R)` and donor-assignment disturbance at most `H(alpha_R+alpha_-)`.
- GC2mk: a residual deficit `delta` has only support-caused roots; one touched primitive anchors at least `ceil(delta/H)` roots.
