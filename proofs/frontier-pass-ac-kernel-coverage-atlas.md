## Logical gain

The previous restricted-kernel pass supplied one coherent physical kernel but did not explain how several kernels can be enlarged and combined without double-counting resources or treating local dead prefixes as global failures.

For a declared physical epoch the atlas now measures

\[
U_{\rm pair}=|\Omega\setminus\cup_\alpha\Omega_\alpha|,
\quad
U_{\rm state}=|V\setminus\cup_\alpha V_\alpha|,
\quad
U_{\rm edge}=|E\setminus\cup_\alpha E_\alpha|.
\]

Adding a sound template decreases these integers by its exact new coverage. A state that is dead in its canonical local template may still be rescued by an explicit union path. Cross-template movement must be a certified physical edge and must have zero-mass activation or a funded reset.

All payment, ticket, reset and disturbance stocks are summed over globally unique physical addresses. Repeated references from several templates are aliases and count once. This prevents source, certificate and ticket mass from being multiplied by the number of menu descriptions that cite it.

On a complete atlas the union graph receives one exact minimax rank and one mixed-radix potential. The episode bound is

\[
E_{\rm atl}
\le
(C_{\rm reset}+1)\Psi_*
+UW_{\rm dist}
+C_{\rm pay}
+C_{\rm ticket},
\]

with all capacities globally deduplicated.
