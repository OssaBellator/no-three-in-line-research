# Frontier pass: source-star resource capacity

- SRR2aw partitions one labeled source-hole star by least physical payment resource.
- SRR2ax extracts a distinct-resource maximum-endpoint stock retaining at least `1/mu` of the star weight.
- SRR2ay gives the overloaded-resource versus independently payable endpoint-stock dichotomy.
- SRR2az composes this with the `1/(|A|K)` labeled-star bound and the exceptional-weight threshold.

The remaining SRR3 work is to prove actual resource multiplicity and payment efficiencies for switching-menu stars, or pay one overloaded blocker, line, owner, boundary or protected resource.
