\[
\boxed{\Delta\Psi(Q)=N_B(Q)-T.}

\[

## Corrections retained

- Canonical IDs, signatures and line profiles are not semantic quotients.
- Fibre linkage proves identity, not transition or state semantics.
- A zero-rank-three response is not a complete background selector.
- The raw selector need not minimize the full background-dependent score.
- Rollback changes the operation; it is not deletion credit in the original row.
- Deterministic, rollback and uniform policies are not interchangeable.
- Literal deltas require actual point, removal and insertion data.
- Evidence strings do not prove deletion, domination, transfer or auxiliary strictness.
- Passing finite checkers proves only their stated equalities.

linked-fibre data, proved operation and fate semantics, strict recurrent certificates
