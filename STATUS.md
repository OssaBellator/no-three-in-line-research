## Corrections retained

- Canonical IDs, signatures, residuals, affine chambers and Pareto vectors are not semantic quotients.
- Source-dependent fibre IDs cannot define their own expected population universe.
- Operation-slot completeness requires an independently derived parent-rule registry.
- Necessary signature inequalities are not a realizability characterization.
- Equal scalar selectors do not imply equal labelled offspring vectors.
- A positive-weight minimizer is Pareto-minimal; positive support is not recurrent contraction.
- A Pareto vector may be convexly unsupported by every strictly positive linear weight.
- The hard core requires all 20 full-selector chambers, not only 11 raw rank-three minima.
- Rollback changes the operation; it is not deletion credit in the original row.
- Occurrence correction, rollback, selector penalty, policy penalty and uniform slack are distinct.
- Literal strictness requires the actual destroyed threshold and survivor relation.
- Passing finite checkers proves only their stated equalities.
