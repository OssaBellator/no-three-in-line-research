## Absolute owner and total fate map

Fix a strict total order on all extendable response edges.  For a witness `w` with
response prescription `P(w)`, define

\[
\operatorname{own}(w)=\max_\prec P(w).
\]

This owner is unique.  The owner/fate manifest requires every raw witness exactly
once and assigns one explicit fate:

1. retained in a recurrent child with the same owner;
2. deleted with a correction-evidence identifier;
3. transferred to a lower-stratum off-diagonal or auxiliary child; or
4. dominated by an explicit positive upper multiplicity in the same owner class.

`check_geometric_owner_fate_manifest.py` recomputes the raw witness family and
owner partition, rejects silent omission and exports the labelled integer
coefficient bins.

Its deterministic self-test validates 400 systems containing 5,586 witnesses and
rejects eleven corrupted manifests.

The checker validates owner and fate syntax.  It does **not** prove that a named
deletion, domination or auxiliary-transfer evidence item is mathematically valid.

## Corrections retained

- Historical target lines, selectors, traces and destroyed loads are not one
  simultaneous current family.
- Finite scheduling and first-use resource termination are not potential
  improvement.
- Rank-mass conservation concerns distinct prescriptions; geometric candidate
  multiplicity is separate.
- Background triples become payment only through current labelled congestion maps.
- Matching-level symmetry does not preserve Euclidean collinearity.
- One geometric representative cannot replace a matching fibre without exact
  equivalence or honest domination.
- Owner-support closure applies only when every retained recurrent child has the
  claimed owner support.
- A transferred witness requires a proved structural transition or certified
  auxiliary module; a role string is not that proof.
- Silent witness deletion is invalid.
- Passing the owner/fate checker proves witness completeness and owner syntax, not
  rule-specific evidence validity.
- Passing the source bundle proves exact coefficient linkage, not semantic
  correctness of the fate evidence.
- Passing the assignment checker proves arithmetic relative to the verified
  coefficient table, not the geometric correctness of an unverified table.
- Auxiliary resolvent elimination applies only after a genuine strict certificate.
- Failure of a peeled or Pareto upper model may indicate coarse domination, not
  supercriticality of the exact row.
