## Structural and spectral endpoint

Every new physical triple has one absolute last-entering edge owner.  Product,
wall, routing, host and envelope exits form a finite owner DAG.  After
ordering the owners topologically, the complete offspring matrix is block upper
triangular:

\[
\boxed{\rho(A)=\max_i\rho(A_{ii}).}
\]

Only genuinely recurrent same-owner blocks require numerical subcriticality.
Finite structural descent, finite resource consumption and scheduler
termination are not by themselves potential improvement.

CMR1486--CMR1493 refine the transfer DAG by recording envelope exponent,
first-separation depth, used absolute tokens and the monotone residual mask.
Strict scaling, earlier-depth handoff, first token use, first private-edge use
and ordinary structural exits are off-diagonal.  Once recurrent core blocks
have rational certificates, all finite off-diagonal collateral glues
constructively into one certificate `Av<v`.

\[
H_e=K_{d,d}\setminus(O\cup\{e\}).
\]

\[
p_e(a)=\Pr(a\in R),
\qquad

\[
\boxed{\mathbb E N(R)=\sum_a p_e(a)g_e(a).}

## Extension-free line cleaning

CMR1510--CMR1517 prove a sharp matching-resilience fact.  Normalize `O` to the
identity and let a Hall-cut core have source size `k` and target size
`z=d-k+1`.  If

\[
m=\min\{k,z\},
\qquad
M=\max\{k,z\},
\]

then every cut satisfies

\[
\boxed{|Q|\ge m+1.}
\]

A partial matching inside the same rectangle has at most `m` edges.  Therefore
no Hall-cut blocker is a partial matching and, for every partial matching

\[
X\subseteq E(H_e),
\]

one has

\[
\boxed{\operatorname{PM}(H_e\setminus X)\ne\varnothing.}
\]

Every nonaxis real line is a partial matching.  Consequently the complete
allowed response trace of any nonaxis line can be deleted while continuing to
avoid `O` and `e`.  The resulting physical line contains only fixed `O` cells,
so it creates no new line-local collateral.

Applied to a CMR1461 loaded owner line, this gives a target-safe response with
zero same-owner, same-line offspring.  Off-line offspring of that response are
still part of the numerical quotient.

## Repeated-token atomic compression

Fix a nonroot token

\[
\tau=(b,a,c,\theta),
\qquad
1\le b<h,
\]

in an envelope `t=p^h`.  Suppose `J` episodes each expose at least `H`
unavailable token edges.  Once one central edge is fixed, the exact stocks are

\[
\boxed{N_{\rm pair}^{e}(t)=(t-1)^2,}
\qquad
\boxed{N_{\rm tr}^{e}(t)=2(t-1).}
\]

Choose integers `q,R>=1` and set

\[
\lambda=2Rq(t-1)^2.
\]

CMR1518--CMR1525 give at least one of:

1. the finite episode bound
   \[
   J\le
   \left\lfloor
   \frac{(\lambda-1)t^2}{Hp^{2b}}
   \right\rfloor;
   \]
2. at least `R` reintroductions of one labelled edge, paying at least
   \[
   R(p+1)(h-1)
   \]
   exact full-token incidences;
3. adaptive absorption of the persistent edge;
4. at least `q` repetitions of one jointly persistent paid-pair selector with
   deterministic restoration surcharge two;
5. at least `q` repetitions of one fixed trace-line or row/column incidence
   signature.

Thus repeated-token and reused-support recurrence compresses to only three
atomic numerical rows:

- labelled edge return;
- persistent paid-pair selector;
- fixed trace-line incidence.

Fresh token-edge stock and successful absorption are not extra recurrent rows.

owner weights, inherited-coordinate capacity, packed translations, private
resource payments, acyclic transfer gluing, root channels, owner-disjoint
