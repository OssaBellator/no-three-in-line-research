# Status and honesty ledger

**Last updated:** 26 July 2026

## External status

The classical no-three-in-line conjecture

\[
D(n)=2n
\]

remains open. This repository does **not** contain a complete proof.

## Closed prime-power components

### Algebra, recursive banks, and height cleaning

- Nonlinear completed-reciprocal channels exist at every odd prime power.
- Balanced recursive banks exist for every prime power with
  \(p\equiv1\pmod4\) and for every power of seven.
- First-separation summation gives expected syndrome
  \(O_p(N^2\log N)\).
- Prefix and joint-parent collateral, Hall-wall peeling, line-clean paid-pair
  banks, exact high-slice cleaning, and harmonic-packet completion are proved at
  their stated scales.
- All relevant intermediate-height dyadic bands partition into at most
  \[
  \left\lceil\frac{1+\log_2t}{2}\right\rceil
  \]
  exactly cleanable harmonic packets.

### Token, packet, and selected-state accounting

- One recursive ancestor or one one-layer whole-parent reset returns at most
  \(t/p^b\) edges of a full token at depth \(b\); one ordered joint-parent reset
  returns at most \(2t/p^b\).
- One prefix pass plus one packet sweep has labelled incidence
  \[
  (p+1)t(h-1)\bigl(h+P_\eta(t)\bigr)=O_p(t\log^2t).
  \]
- Exact selected-state cycles are erasable under a monotone forbidden mask.
- Distinct feasible matchings pay at least two entering and two leaving edges.
- Recreated selected conflicts contain entering edges; entering and leaving
  churn have equal cardinality.
- Lossy packet resets pay permanent deletion or reach a fully forced terminal
  certificate, with polynomial installation and ancestry bounds.

### Exchange corridors, rollback, and minimum-cost faces

- All essential edges lie in one matching core of size at most \(t\).
- First-essentiality layers are directed exchange corridors with exact
  Dilworth cycle-cover width and linear temporal compression.
- Every final essential edge has a common-epoch rollback using at most \(t\)
  deleted edges.
- A minimum rollback footprint is a forced matching and factors the avoiding
  host to lower side length.
- Restoring an edge set \(R\) has exact labelled token incidence
  \[
  (p+1)(h-1)|R|.
  \]
- Minimum rollback is a binary-cost assignment problem. The optimum family is
  the exact tight host, has potentials in \([-k,0]\), factors over strongly
  connected exchange blocks, and has at most \(2k\) cross-level edges.
- Conditional on the sparse level skeleton, the remaining optimum family
  factors by potential level. Same-level marked motion factors or lies on a
  mixed-colour zero-cost cycle.

### Mixed-cycle, theta-fan, and line-clean geometry

- Mixed-cycle witnesses pack vertex-disjointly, concentrate through a boundary
  vertex/edge, or disappear after sparse boundary deletion.
- Directed Menger yields theta fans or small return cuts. Theta-fan entering
  sets have one common edge and private route edges.
- Rooted conflicts form secant stars or compatible rank-two pairs.
- Every compatible paid pair has an exact line-clean derangement cylinder.
- Weighted averaging closes one-shot line-clean selection: failure gives fixed
  collateral or unavailable-edge inventory.
- Adaptive matching absorption, König covers, persistent crosses, temporal
  token ledgers, and refined trace ancestry reduce repeated failures to finite
  stock, reintroduction payment, or a fixed compatible-pair selector.

### Canonical selectors and protected execution

- Every selector has one canonical forbidden matching, fixed collateral profile,
  fixed allowed-edge universe, and owner-labelled edge/line/token stock.
- Threshold-one dynamic selectors have the uniform near-static bound
  \(S\ge77/480\).
- Persistent unavailable contacts amplify to jointly absent cores, batch
  absorption, or simultaneous row/column token walls.
- Heavy lines absorb all cells outside the protected core.
- Secant stars yield a matching-vertex wall or one simultaneous compatible arm
  absorption batch.
- Fresh protected absorption is bounded by residual matching capacity.

### Sparse products and essential recursion

- A large protected core leaves a balanced interface of at most twice the free
  side.
- Fixing the interface gives an exact protected-factor/free-factor product.
- Candidate conflicts decompose into pure factor conflicts and sparse mixed
  Cartesian rectangles of rank at most two in either factor.
- Nonessential prescriptions pay matching-preserving deletion. Essential
  prescriptions contract exactly and transfer to the other factor.
- Forced product certificates can disappear only through certificate-edge
  deletion, skeleton churn, or genuinely entering factor edges on affected
  alternating components.
- Contracting the complete essential core leaves a residual host with no
  essential edges. Core-anchored rank-one/rank-two conflicts enter a finite
  deletion/contraction recursion.

### Strict prime-power child recursion

- Every reduced factor has one canonical deepest full-prefix envelope and one
  first nontrivial child split.
- Every factor matching has an exact \(p\times p\) child transport table.
- Fixing vertex routing gives an exact product over strict child prefix hosts,
  with at most \(p^{2d}\) routing skeletons for factor side \(d\).
- Every routing change has at least two entering and two leaving physical support
  edges. Nonrecurrent routing changes satisfy
  \[
  R\le\left\lfloor\frac{(\lambda-1)d^2}{2}\right\rfloor,
  \]
  and carry exact full-token incidence.
- Inside one fixed child product, every active cross-child conflict has rank
  vector \((2,1)\) or \((1,1,1)\) and an exact occurrence box.
- A nonforced active mixed atom contains a nonessential child edge. Deleting it
  preserves a product matching and cannot activate a new atom.
- After at most \(d^2\) mixed deletions, the product is globally clean, exposes
  a forced essential cross-child certificate, or reduces to one pure-dirty
  strict child factor of side at most \(d-1\).
- Strict child recursion decreases both envelope side and factor side.
- Forced child certificates have polynomial owner-stage stock. Their escape is
  paid by certificate-edge deletion, routing churn, or entering-edge support.

## Important corrections

- Naive sequential two-layer rematching may reoccupy an old first-layer cell.
- Static token consumption is not monotone; exact token-restoring cycles exist.
- Recreated conflicts are supported by entering edges, not leaving edges.
- Historical alternating cycles are not automatically executable in the final
  host; sparse rollback and minimum-cost normalisation are the valid temporal
  replacements.
- Theta-fan cycles sharing a boundary edge are structural alternatives, not a
  simultaneous-flip family.
- Relabelling a forbidden matching as diagonal is combinatorial only;
  collinearity is always evaluated in original parent-grid coordinates.
- One new edge may unlock several essential edges on the same alternating cycle;
  payment is per affected component, not per omitted essential edge.
- Routing compensation may be distributed across several alternating cycles;
  CMR673 charges their union and does not assert one cycle carries all changes.

## Current open frontier

1. **Descending-path potential aggregation.** Combine the finite mixed-deletion,
   strict-child, forced-certificate, and routing-edge ledgers with inherited
   target-load descent and protected-reserve depletion.
2. **Envelope-budget closure.** Prove that a path which does not obtain a clean
   state or reserve decrease must consume full-token return, deletion ancestry,
   or one of the at most \(h\) strict closure-envelope expansions.
3. **Prime-field and low-height transfer.** Integrate the remaining thin
   quotient/carry signatures and rebuild the owner-labelled endpoint for prime
   fields outside the balanced recursive families.
4. **Arbitrary side lengths.** Extend balanced prime families, control CRT
   projections, and cover every positive integer \(n\).

## Bottom line

There is no complete proof. Through **CMR690**, line-clean selection, dynamic
availability, protected-certificate execution, sparse-interface products,
product-conflict rectangles, essential-core recursion, routing-change payment,
finite mixed-child deletion, strict child descent, and forced-child ancestry are
closed at their stated scales.

The principal remaining prime-power theorem is a global potential/budget
aggregation over the now strictly descending and owner-labelled execution path.
Arbitrary side-length coverage remains necessary afterward.
