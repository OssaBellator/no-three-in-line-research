# Condensed research log

This log records the evolution of the project.

1. **Reservoir framework:** remove a set, complete row/column deficits through a clone graph, and control external secants.
2. **Pair-shadow regularization:** identify retained anchors as unique witnesses for two-new-one-old triples.
3. **Candidate-only obstruction:** prove logarithmic line energy, ruling out simple first-moment pruning.
4. **Clustered switching:** formulate line-level repair banks and pair/triple conflict certificates.
5. **Reverse scales:** process primitive heights downward and introduce certified multicovers.
6. **Uniform local bank:** prove the spread-selection implication under explicit shadow caps.
7. **Tomographic trades:** use difference operators to preserve finite protected directions exactly.
8. **Affine terminal constructions:** solve finite-direction saturation for arithmetic classes of \(n\).
9. **Subgroup absorbers:** install linearly many exact protected block states.
10. **Exact block collateral:** show high lines meet small blocks once, yielding an exact state-average formula.
11. **Block closure:** move whole endpoint blocks into the reservoir.
12. **Wall route:** derive finite-geometry wall obstructions, then refute synchronized termination with translated blocks.
13. **Product CSP:** formulate independent block states and a local-lemma completion criterion.
14. **Hyperbola pathway:** construct saturated two-channel seeds with line cap four and \(O(n\log n)\) syndrome.
15. **Hamiltonian interleaver:** interpret the two channels as one or more even cycles.
16. **Orbit codebooks:** preserve bounded-hyperbola geometry under indefinitely many state changes.
17. **Möbius syndrome graph:** show each opposite-channel anchor induces an involution matching.
18. **Cycle trades:** every residual core cycle supports a cyclic matching bank.
19. **Window products:** quantify the conic complexity of each cycle state.
20. **Current target:** prove or disprove carry-cycle dispersion.
